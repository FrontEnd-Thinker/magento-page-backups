The Sophos Virtual Firewall at a Glance

Deployment

Runs on VMware, Microsoft Hyper-V, KVM, and Citrix. You can also install the SFOS software image on your own Intel server. Deploy from the provided image, assign up to 8 vCPU cores, and give it as much RAM as the host allows.

Base License

Perpetual and included with the appliance. Covers firewalling, networking, wireless, unlimited remote access and site-to-site VPN, the Xstream architecture, and reporting with Sophos Central cloud management.

Protection Bundles

Standard adds Network Protection (next-gen IPS, ATP, Security Heartbeat, SD-RED VPN) and Web Protection. Xstream includes everything in Standard plus Zero-Day Protection and Central Orchestration.

Support & Terms

Every bundle includes Enhanced Support with 24/7 access and feature updates. Protection subscriptions are available in 1, 3, and 5 year terms, with the longer terms carrying the best value.
