Fortinet 3200E. Carrier-Class Email Security with 10GbE Connectivity

The Fortinet FortiMail 3200E is Fortinet's flagship email security appliance from the E-series line, purpose-built for the largest universities, enterprises, ISPs, and carriers. It shares the 3000E's 2U chassis and 3,000-mailbox capacity but adds dual 10GbE SFP+ interfaces and the highest throughput in the family — up to 3.4 million messages per hour. Dual hot-swappable power supplies and hardware RAID-protected storage keep mail flowing without interruption in the most demanding, high-volume environments.
