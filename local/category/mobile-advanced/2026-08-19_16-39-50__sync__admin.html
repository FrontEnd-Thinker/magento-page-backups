Central Mobile Advanced at a Glance

Unified endpoint management

Enroll and manage corporate and personal devices across iOS, Android, Chrome OS, Windows 10, and macOS from one system. Push apps, set configuration, and enforce compliance so every device meets the same security policy regardless of who owns it.

Mobile threat defense

Advanced adds Intercept X for Mobile, built on the same deep learning engine as Sophos endpoint protection. It guards against malware, unwanted apps, malicious websites, and man-in-the-middle attacks, and can automatically restrict access when a device falls out of compliance.

Work data stays separate

Deploy corporate email and documents into a secure container so business data lives apart from personal photos, messages, and apps. Admins keep control of company content without touching a user's private data, which is what makes BYOD workable.

One place for everything

Sophos Central manages mobile right next to your endpoint, server, firewall, and email security. Feed mobile threat and device data into Sophos XDR to hunt across your whole environment and respond faster from a single console.

Standard or Advanced?

Central Mobile Standard covers device management on its own. Advanced includes everything in Standard plus Intercept X for Mobile threat defense and web protection. If you want the security layer and not just the management, Advanced is the one.
