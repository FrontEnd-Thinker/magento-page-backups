AP6 420X at a Glance

Built for outside

An IP67-rated enclosure keeps out dust and water, so the 420X holds up to rain, heat, and cold. It mounts to a pole or exterior wall and ships with external omnidirectional antennas.

Wi-Fi 6 performance

802.11ax across the 2.4 and 5 GHz bands with a 2x2:2 radio design and WPA3 security. Good coverage for open outdoor areas and busy, device-heavy spaces alike.

Power & uplink

A single 2.5G interface with PoE+ (802.3at) carries data and power over one cable. Note the plain SKU ships hardware only, so plan a PoE+ injector or switch to power it.

Managed in Sophos Central

Register the AP and run it from the same cloud console as your Sophos firewalls and switches. A per-AP support subscription unlocks Central management, firmware, and 24/7 support.

Not sure the 420X is the right AP6?

The outdoor 420X is one of five AP6 models. If you are covering indoor offices, high-density rooms, or want the 6 GHz band, another model may fit better. Line them up side by side.
