Fortinet 243K. High-Density Wi-Fi 7 Access Point with External Antennas

The FortiAP 243K is a premium indoor Wi-Fi 7 access point built for the toughest RF environments — auditoriums, stadium concourses, and warehouses — where six external RP-SMA antenna connectors let you attach directional or high-gain antennas tuned to the space. Four radios (2.4/5/6 GHz plus a dedicated scanning radio) and a 10G multigigabit uplink give it substantially more headroom than internal-antenna models like the 231K. Full 4-radio performance calls for 802.3bt PoE, making planning your power budget an important first step.
