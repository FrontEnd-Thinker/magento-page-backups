Rack Mount Kits at a Glance

Compatibility

SonicWall matches a specific SKU to each TZ chassis: 01-SSC-0742 for TZ300/350, 01-SSC-0438 for TZ500 series, 02-SSC-3112/3116 for TZ570/670, and 03-SSC-3457 for TZ380/480/580/680.

What's Included

Mounting brackets sized to your TZ chassis plus the screws needed to attach them to the appliance and your rack rails.

Installation

Brackets bolt to the sides of the appliance, then screw into standard rack rails — a screwdriver is all you need.

Rack Space Planning

Each kit uses 1U. SonicWall doesn't sell a shared dual-unit tray for the TZ desktop line, so plan one rack slot per firewall.
