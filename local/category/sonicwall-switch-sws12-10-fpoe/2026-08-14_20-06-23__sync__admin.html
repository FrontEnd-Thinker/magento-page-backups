SonicWall Switch 12-10-FPoE. Full PoE+ Desktop Switching for Demanding Devices.

The SonicWall Switch 12-10-FPoE is a 10-port gigabit desktop switch delivering 802.3af/at PoE+ across 8 copper ports with up to 130 watts of total PoE budget and 30 watts per port. That makes it capable of powering high-performance wireless access points, PTZ cameras, and other PoE+ devices that standard 802.3af switches cannot support. Two 1G SFP uplinks, a built-in 180W power supply, and 24 Gbps of switching capacity round out this compact powerhouse for SD-Branch deployments.

Switch 12-10-FPoE at a Glance

Full PoE+ Power

802.3af/at PoE+ on 8 copper ports with up to 30W per port and 130W total budget. Powers high-demand devices including Wi-Fi 6 access points, PTZ cameras, and video conferencing endpoints that require more than the 15.4W limit of standard 802.3af.

Performance

24 Gbps non-blocking switching capacity across 10 x 1G copper ports and 2 x 1G SFP uplinks. 256MB memory, 32MB flash, 512KB packet buffer, and 8K MAC table keep traffic flowing smoothly in small office environments.

Management

Managed via SonicWall firewalls, Wireless Network Manager, or the switch's standalone web GUI and CLI. Supports per-port PoE scheduling, power priority, user-defined power limits, SNMP monitoring, SSH, SNTP/NTP, and zero-touch deployment.

Power and Form Factor

Built-in 180W fixed power supply (100-240 VAC, 50-60 Hz). Maximum power consumption: 152.8W. Desktop form factor with one cooling fan: 330 x 230 x 44mm, 2.5 kg. MTBF rated at over 180,000 hours.
