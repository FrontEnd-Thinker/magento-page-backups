Adaptive Defense 360 Mobile at a Glance

Web and Phishing Protection

Blocks malicious URLs, phishing pages, and fraudulent sites in real time across all browsers on iOS and Android. Employees are protected whether they are browsing on Safari, Chrome, or any other mobile browser, with no VPN or proxy workaround needed.

App Scanning and Risk Assessment

Scans installed applications for known malware and privacy risks on Android devices. Alerts administrators when high-risk or non-compliant apps are detected, enabling enforcement of app policies without requiring full MDM enrollment for every use case.

Compliance and Device Checks

Verifies device security posture including OS version, screen lock status, jailbreak or root detection, and encryption state. Non-compliant devices can trigger automated alerts or policy actions, reducing the risk that a compromised phone becomes an entry point into your network.

Unified Aether Management

Mobile devices are managed from the same Aether console used for desktop and server endpoints. A single policy dashboard covers all platforms, so administrators do not need to context-switch between separate portals to maintain consistent coverage across the full device fleet.

The Panda Adaptive Defense 360 Mobile agent is distributed through the Apple App Store for iOS devices and Google Play for Android devices. Administrators send an enrollment invitation from the Aether console, and employees install the app and accept the enrollment link. The process takes only a few minutes per device and does not require physical access to the phone or tablet.

Adaptive Defense 360 Mobile supports iOS 12 and later on Apple devices and Android 5.0 (Lollipop) and later on Android devices. Most modern business smartphones and tablets fall well within this range. Contact a Firewalls.com specialist if you have specific device models that need compatibility verification before purchasing licenses.

The AD360 Mobile agent is designed to run as a lightweight background process. Web threat protection uses cloud lookups rather than on-device scanning, which keeps the local processing footprint minimal. Battery and performance impact is comparable to other business security apps and should not be noticeable under normal usage conditions.
