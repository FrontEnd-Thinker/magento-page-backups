Fortinet 320C. Legacy Indoor 802.11ac Access Point

The FortiAP 320C is a dual-radio, 3x3 MIMO indoor access point supporting 802.11a/n/ac and 802.11b/g/n with association rates up to 1,300 Mbps. Its sealed, plenum-rated enclosure and dual redundant PoE Ethernet ports made it a durable pick for warehouses, above-ceiling installs, and mission-critical office deployments. Note: the FortiAP 320C is a legacy model that has reached Fortinet's end-of-sale/end-of-support milestones, so it's best suited for replacing an existing 320C in a matched deployment rather than new rollouts.

320C FAQ

The FortiAP 320C is an indoor access point. Its sealed, plenum-rated enclosure allows it to be mounted above ceiling tiles or in non-climate-controlled indoor spaces like warehouses, but it is not rated for outdoor use.

The 320C is a legacy FortiAP model that has already passed its published end-of-sale and end-of-support milestones. It's best used to replace or expand an existing 320C deployment; for new rollouts, consider a current-generation FortiAP like the 321E, 421E, or 431F.

The 320C has dual-redundant 802.3af/802.3at PoE Ethernet ports, letting you connect two PoE sources for failover in mission-critical deployments.

Yes. Like other FortiAP models, the 320C can be centrally managed by the integrated WLAN controller on a FortiGate firewall, or through Fortinet's cloud-based provisioning portal.

The 320C is a dual-radio 802.11ac access point that also supports 802.11a/b/g/n, delivering association rates up to 1,300 Mbps with 3x3 MIMO. It predates 802.11ac Wave 2 and Wi-Fi 6 models.
