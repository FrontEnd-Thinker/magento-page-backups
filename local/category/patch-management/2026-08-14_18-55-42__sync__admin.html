Patch Management at a Glance

Vulnerability Discovery

Continuously scans all managed endpoints for missing patches and known vulnerabilities. Provides a prioritized view of outstanding patches, categorized by severity (critical, high, medium, low), so IT teams can focus remediation effort on the highest-risk exposures first.

OS and Third-Party Patching

Deploys Windows OS updates and patches for more than 500 third-party applications including browsers, Java, Adobe, VPN clients, and productivity software. Third-party application patching is where many organizations have the largest unmanaged exposure.

Flexible Deployment Scheduling

Schedule patch deployments during maintenance windows to avoid disrupting business operations. Define exclusion periods, reboot policies, and retry behavior. Deploy patches immediately to specific devices for emergency vulnerability response without waiting for the next scheduled cycle.

Patch Status Reporting

Dashboard and report views show patch compliance percentage by device, device group, or application. Generate audit-ready reports demonstrating that critical patches were applied within defined timeframes, supporting compliance requirements for frameworks such as PCI DSS and HIPAA.

Once you confirm the patches applied successfully and have not caused any issues, you can approve the broader deployment to production device groups. This staged approach reduces the risk of a bad patch disrupting operations across your entire fleet at once.

When a device is offline during a scheduled patch window, Panda Patch Management queues the pending patches and deploys them the next time the device comes online and connects to the Aether platform. You can configure retry behavior and set an expiration for how long a queued patch remains active, ensuring that devices that rejoin the network after an extended absence are brought into compliance automatically.
