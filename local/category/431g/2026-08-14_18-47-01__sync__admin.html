431G FAQ

No. The FortiAP 431G is an indoor-rated access point with no IP ingress rating; for outdoor or industrial deployments, Fortinet offers the ruggedized FortiAP 432F/432FR series.

The 431G is a Wi-Fi 6E (802.11ax) tri-radio access point that adds the 6 GHz band on top of standard 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz Wi-Fi 6, enabling less-congested, higher-capacity connections.

It ships to run on 802.3bt PoE by default, and also supports dual 802.3at PoE inputs for current sharing across its two 5GbE ports.

It's centrally managed through the integrated wireless controller on any FortiGate firewall, or through the cloud-based FortiLAN Cloud provisioning and management portal.

Combined across its three radios, the 431G can reach up to 1.148 Gbps on 2.4 GHz, 2.402 Gbps on 5 GHz, and 4.804 Gbps on the 6 GHz radio.
