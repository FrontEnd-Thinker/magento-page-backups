S223E at a Glance

Wireless Performance

802.11ac Wave 2 dual-radio design, 2.4GHz and 5GHz concurrent.
Up to 300 Mbps on 2.4GHz and 867 Mbps on 5GHz (~1.14 Gbps aggregate).
MU-MIMO, transmit beamforming, and LDPC for reliable multi-device throughput.

Antennas & Coverage

4 external dipole antennas (4 dBi @2.4GHz, 5 dBi @5GHz) that can be repositioned for coverage tuning.
Omnidirectional design for standard indoor offices and classrooms — not a sector/directional antenna model.
Indoor use only; no IP ingress rating.

Onboard Security

Embedded UTM engine delivers IPS, antivirus, web filtering, and application control at the access point.
WIDS/WIPS and rogue AP scanning run in background or full-time mode on the standard data radios.
Extends FortiGate-grade protection to remote or unmanaged sites.

Power & Management

Dual redundant PoE ports (802.3af/802.3at); optional AC adapter or Fortinet power injector.
Managed via an integrated FortiGate WLAN controller or FortiCloud.
Supports Local-Bridge, Mesh, and Tunnel SSID modes, up to 16 SSIDs.
