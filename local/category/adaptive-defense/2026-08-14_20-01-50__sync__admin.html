Adaptive Defense at a Glance

EDR and Threat Hunting

Continuous monitoring of all endpoint activity with full forensic timelines, attack chain visualization, and automated response options. Panda's threat hunting team reviews alerts 24/7 and sends detailed incident reports when real threats are found.

Zero-Trust Application Service

Machine learning classifies every process as trusted, malicious, or unknown before it can execute freely. Unknown applications are sandboxed and held for analysis, eliminating the gray area that attackers exploit with fileless and living-off-the-land techniques.

Indicator of Attack Detection

Adaptive Defense maps activity to the MITRE ATT&CK framework, surfacing tactics and techniques used by known threat groups. Security teams can query this context to understand attack scope and prioritize response efforts.

Cloud Management

The Aether console provides a single view of every protected device, active threats, and investigation status. No on-premise servers required. Lightweight agent deployable via GPO, RMM, or manual installer across Windows, macOS, and Linux.
