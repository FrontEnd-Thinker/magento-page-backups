XGS 4300 at a Glance

Performance

Firewall throughput: 75 Gbps
IPS throughput: 29.5 Gbps
Threat protection: 6.5 Gbps
NGFW: 23 Gbps
Xstream TLS inspection: 8 Gbps

Connectivity

Fixed ports: 4 x GE copper, 4 x 2.5GbE copper, 4 x SFP+ 10GbE fiber
Expansion: Flexi Port module bay for copper, fiber, or PoE
Resilience: LAN bypass port pair built in

Xstream Architecture

Dual-processor design pairs a multi-core CPU with a dedicated Xstream Flow Processor. Benign and encrypted flows ride the FastPath, so TLS 1.3 inspection and deep packet inspection run without a throughput penalty.

Form Factor & Power

1U rackmount with the mounting kit included. Optional second power supply adds redundancy, and integrated Xstream SD-WAN plus IPsec and SSL VPN handle multi-site connectivity.
