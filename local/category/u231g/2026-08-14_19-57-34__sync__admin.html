U231G at a Glance

Wireless Performance

Tri-radio 802.11ax design across 2.4GHz, 5GHz, and 6GHz, each 2x2:2 MIMO.
Up to 574 Mbps (2.4GHz), 1201 Mbps (5GHz), and 2401 Mbps (6GHz) per-radio PHY rates.
Internal PIFA antennas plus a dedicated BLE/iBeacon radio.

Flexible Power Options

Runs on standard 802.3at PoE+ via an injector such as the GPI-130.
No PoE switch on site? Power it instead with Fortinet's standalone AC adapter (SP-FAP400-PA) from any wall outlet.
Note: there is no confirmed USB-C charger option for this model — power comes from PoE+ or the AC adapter.

Remote & Branch Deployment

Supports Tunnel-mode SSIDs to securely backhaul traffic to a FortiGate over the WAN — ideal for teleworkers and branch sites.
Local-Bridge and Mesh SSID modes also supported.
Indoor use only; no IP ingress rating.

Connectivity & Management

2 RJ45 ports (2.5GbE + 1GbE) plus RS-232 console and USB Type-A 3.0 for peripherals.
Managed via an integrated FortiGate WLAN controller or FortiLAN Cloud, with zero-touch provisioning for remote sites.
Up to 24 SSIDs, WPA3, built-in WIDS/WIPS.
