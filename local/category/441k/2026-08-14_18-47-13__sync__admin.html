441K at a Glance

Wireless Performance

Wi-Fi 7 (802.11be) quad-radio design with dedicated 2.4 GHz, 5 GHz, 6 GHz, and 2x2 scanning radios.
4x4 MU-MIMO with OFDMA, BSS Coloring, and Target Wake Time for extreme client density.
Data rates up to 1.148 Gbps (2.4 GHz), 8.648 Gbps (5 GHz), and 11.53 Gbps (6 GHz).

Connectivity & Power

Dual 10 Gigabit Ethernet (RJ45) ports to keep pace with multi-gig Wi-Fi 7 throughput.
Powered by 802.3bt PoE, or dual 802.3at PoE for current sharing.
Integrated GNSS/GPS antenna alongside BLE/Zigbee for locationing and precision timing.

Indoor Deployment

Purpose-built indoor access point rated for standard commercial environments (0°C to 50°C).
Ceiling, T-rail, and wall mounting options with included mount kit.
UL2043 plenum-rated for above-ceiling installations.

Management & Security

Centrally managed through any FortiGate integrated wireless controller or the FortiLAN Cloud portal.
Built-in Firewall, IPS, Application Control, and Web Filtering extend the Security Fabric to the wireless edge.
Wi-Fi Alliance certified, with Wi-Fi 7 certification in progress.
