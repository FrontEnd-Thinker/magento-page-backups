NSv 470 at a Glance

Performance

Up to 17 Gbps firewall inspection with 4.5 Gbps of full threat prevention. Performance scales with the vCPU and memory resources allocated in your hypervisor or cloud instance.

Platform Support

Deploy on VMware ESXi, Microsoft Hyper-V, and KVM on-premises, or as an instance in AWS, Azure, or Google Cloud. A single license model covers all supported platforms.

Security Services

Full AGSS support including Gateway Anti-Virus, IPS, Application Control, Content Filtering, and Capture ATP. TLS 1.3 inspection is included without performance cliffs.

Management

Managed identically to physical SonicWall appliances through SonicOS 7.0 and NSM. REST API support enables infrastructure-as-code workflows and zero-touch provisioning.
