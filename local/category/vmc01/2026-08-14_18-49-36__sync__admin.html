FortiWeb VMC01 FAQ

The Fortinet FortiWeb VMC01 is a Docker container image of FortiWeb, built for container-native and DevOps-driven environments. It delivers up to 25 Mbps of protected WAF throughput without requiring a full virtual machine.

FortiWeb-VMC is packaged specifically as a Docker container image — it deploys via Docker Engine rather than a hypervisor or VM image, fitting directly into containerized CI/CD pipelines.

No. High availability clustering is not supported on the FortiWeb-VMC container line. If HA/failover is required, use the equivalent FortiWeb-VM tier instead.

4 GB minimum RAM (8 GB recommended) across all VMC tiers, with 30 GB minimum / 500 GB maximum storage.

Both share the same throughput tier and WAF feature set, but FortiWeb-VMC is Docker-only and does not support HA clustering, while FortiWeb-VM deploys on hypervisors or public clouds and does support HA. Choose VMC for lightweight, container-orchestrated deployments; choose VM when you need failover clustering.
