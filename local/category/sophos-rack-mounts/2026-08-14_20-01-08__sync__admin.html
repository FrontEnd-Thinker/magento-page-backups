Rack Mount Kits at a Glance

Compatibility

RM-SR-T11 fits the XGS 87 and 107. RM-SR-T12 fits the larger XGS 116, 118, 126, 128, 136, and 138. Confirm your exact model and we'll match the tray.

What's Included

A vented shelf, a bracket to secure the external power supply, front port pass-through design, and mounting screws.

Installation

The appliance rests in the slotted tray with no tools required; a screwdriver is only needed to attach the tray to your rack rails. Most installs take under five minutes.

Rack Space Planning

Plan on 1U for the T11, or roughly 1.3U for the taller T12 shelf. No side-by-side dual-unit kit is offered for these models.
