AP6 420E at a Glance

Wi-Fi 6/6E Performance

Tri-radio design covers the 2.4, 5, and 6 GHz bands on the 802.11ax standard. The 6 GHz band is the cleaner, less crowded space that Wi-Fi 6E opens up, which is where the 420E earns its keep in higher-density rooms. Runs a 2x2:2 radio config with WPA3 security.

Uplink & Power

One 2.5G Ethernet port keeps the AP from becoming the choke point on a fast LAN. Powered over that same cable with PoE+ (802.3at). No power adapter or PoE injector is included in the box, so plan for a PoE+ switch or injector.

Management

Managed in Sophos Central, the same console as your Sophos firewalls, switches, and endpoints. A local web interface is available for setting up a single AP on site. A support subscription per AP is required for Central management.

Deployment & Build

Plenum-rated (UL2043) for indoor desktop, wall, or ceiling mounting, including above drop ceilings. Backed by a limited lifetime warranty, with firmware updates, Advanced RMA, and 24/7 support tied to the AP support contract.
