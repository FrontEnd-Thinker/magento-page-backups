Fortinet 224E. Outdoor 802.11ac Wave 2 Access Point

The FortiAP 224E is a ruggedized IP67 outdoor access point with internal antennas, built to extend secure Wi-Fi coverage across campuses, yards, and other outdoor environments. Beyond standard access, its GbE and SFP ports support outdoor bridging and mesh deployments where running new cable isn't practical. Dual-radio 802.11ac Wave 2 performance and FortiGate-based management make it easy to manage alongside the rest of your Fortinet wireless fleet.
