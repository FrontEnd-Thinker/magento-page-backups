Fortinet 2000E. Large-Enterprise Email Security Gateway

The Fortinet FortiMail 2000E is a 2U rack-mount secure email gateway built for large enterprises, education systems, and government agencies that need enterprise-grade protection against spam, phishing, malware, and business email compromise. With dual hot-swappable power supplies, hardware RAID-protected storage, and support for up to 2,000 server-mode mailboxes, the 2000E is engineered for always-on, high-volume mail environments. It's the entry point into Fortinet's high-end FortiMail hardware lineup, delivering enterprise scale without the throughput demands of a service-provider deployment.

2000E FAQ

In server mode the 2000E is rated for up to 2,000 local mailboxes, with support for 800 protected email domains — sized for large enterprises, school districts, and government agencies.

Fortinet rates the 2000E at up to 1.5 million messages per hour for email routing, dropping to roughly 1.0 million per hour with full antispam and virus-outbreak scanning enabled and 700,000 per hour with Enterprise ATP active.

Yes. It ships with 2x 2TB drives (expandable up to 6x 2TB) behind a hardware RAID controller supporting RAID 1, 5, 10, and 50 with hot-spare drives.

The appliance includes dual hot-swappable power supplies, and FortiMail software supports active-passive and active-active HA pairing between two appliances for full device-level redundancy.

The 2000E, 3000E, and 3200E share the same 2U chassis and RAID architecture, but the 3000E and 3200E scale to more protected domains, more recipient policies, and significantly higher throughput — the 3200E also adds dual 10GbE SFP+ ports for carrier-class connectivity.
