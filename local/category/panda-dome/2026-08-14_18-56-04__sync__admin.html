Panda Dome at a Glance

Four Protection Tiers

Dome Essential delivers core antivirus and Wi-Fi scanning. Dome Advanced adds a personal firewall and anti-ransomware protection. Dome Complete adds parental controls and a password manager. Dome Premium adds a VPN with unlimited bandwidth and unlimited device coverage. Each tier includes everything from the tiers below it.

Collective Intelligence Engine

All Dome tiers use Panda's Collective Intelligence cloud detection engine, which draws threat intelligence from millions of protected endpoints worldwide. New threats are identified and classified in the cloud and pushed to all protected devices in real time, without waiting for signature update downloads.

Cross-Platform, Single Subscription

One Dome subscription covers all of your Windows, macOS, Android, and iOS devices up to the seat count in your plan. Dome Premium removes the device count limit entirely. All devices and settings are managed from the My Account web portal, which works from any browser.

Lightweight and Always On

The Dome agent runs in the background with minimal impact on device performance. Because heavy threat analysis happens in the cloud, local resource usage stays low even during full system scans. The agent updates itself automatically, so protection stays current without user action.

Panda Dome FAQ

Panda Dome Essential includes real-time antivirus and Wi-Fi security scanning. Dome Advanced adds a personal firewall, anti-ransomware behavioral protection, and USB drive scanning. Dome Complete adds parental controls and a password manager on top of Advanced. Dome Premium adds a VPN with unlimited bandwidth, unlimited device coverage, and premium support on top of Complete. Each tier is a superset of the one below it.

Panda Dome is designed for home users and very small teams with straightforward security requirements. It does not include centralized policy management, EDR, compliance reporting, or the device fleet management features found in Panda's business products. Small businesses with more than a handful of devices, compliance obligations, or a need for IT administrator control should evaluate Adaptive Endpoint Protection, Adaptive Defense 360, or a Fusion bundle instead. A Firewalls.com specialist can help you determine the right product for your organization.

From there you can view protection status for each device, add new devices by sending an installation link, adjust parental control settings, access your password vault, and manage your subscription and renewal. No separate management software is needed on your computers.
