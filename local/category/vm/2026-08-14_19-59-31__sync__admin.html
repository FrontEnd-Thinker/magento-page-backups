FortiManager Virtual at a Glance

Licensing

Stackable licenses from FMG-VM-Base (10 devices/VDOMs) up through FMG-VM-5000-UG (5,000+ devices/VDOMs), so capacity grows with your environment.

Platform Support

Runs on VMware ESXi, Microsoft Hyper-V, Citrix XenServer, open source Xen, KVM, Nutanix AHV, and major public clouds including AWS, Azure, GCP, OCI, and AliCloud.

Resource Requirements

Minimum 2 vCPU (unlimited max), 4 GB RAM minimum (unlimited for 64-bit), and 100 GB minimum storage (up to 16 TB).

Best For

Organizations that want FortiManager's full feature set without dedicated hardware, or that need to deploy management infrastructure directly in the cloud.

FortiManager Virtual FAQ

FortiManager VM is a software-based version of the FortiManager hardware appliance, offering the same centralized management, policy, and log analysis features on your choice of hypervisor or cloud platform.

VMware ESX/ESXi, Microsoft Hyper-V, Citrix XenServer, open source Xen, KVM, and Nutanix AHV, along with AWS, Microsoft Azure, Google Cloud, Oracle Cloud Infrastructure, and Alibaba Cloud.

It uses a stackable license model starting with the FMG-VM-Base (10 devices/VDOMs) and scaling through upgrade licenses up to 5,000+ devices/VDOMs per instance.

A minimum of 2 vCPUs, 4 GB of RAM, and 100 GB of storage, though these scale up depending on your license tier and device count.

Yes, FortiManager VM supports HA just like the hardware appliances.
