SonicWall Capture Client FAQ

SonicWall Capture Client is a cloud-delivered endpoint protection platform that integrates with SonicWall firewalls to provide continuous behavioral monitoring, machine learning-driven threat detection, DPI-SSL certificate management, and ransomware rollback capabilities. It is available in two plans: Advanced and Premier.

Capture Client Advanced covers essential endpoint protection including next-generation antimalware, DPI-SSL certificate deployment, content filtering, rollback and remediation, and network security integrations. Capture Client Premier adds active endpoint detection and response (EDR), rogue device detection, endpoint network control, deep visibility threat hunting, and remote shell access on top of everything Advanced includes.

Yes. Capture Client is designed to integrate natively with SonicWall next-generation firewalls. This integration allows the firewall and endpoint agent to share threat intelligence, enforce security policies, and automate responses across both the network perimeter and individual endpoints, giving your security team a unified view of activity across the environment.

Deep Packet Inspection of SSL (DPI-SSL) allows SonicWall firewalls to inspect encrypted traffic for threats. Capture Client simplifies this by automatically deploying and managing the DPI-SSL certificate to every protected endpoint, eliminating the manual certificate distribution that would otherwise be required from IT teams.

Yes. Capture Client Advanced and Premier both include the SentinelOne-powered rollback and remediation feature. If ransomware or other malware modifies or encrypts files on a protected endpoint, Capture Client can revert those changes and restore the system to its pre-attack state without requiring a full reimaging of the machine.

Capture Client is licensed on a per-endpoint, subscription basis. Licenses are available in 1-year, 2-year, and 3-year terms. Organizations choose between the Advanced or Premier plan depending on the level of protection needed, and licenses can scale as the number of endpoints in the environment grows.
