Fortinet 300D. Legacy Data Center Application Delivery Controller (D-Series)

The FortiADC 300D is a previous-generation Fortinet application delivery controller from the D-series lineup, originally built for mid-sized data center server load balancing and SSL offload. According to publicly available Fortinet lifecycle trackers, Fortinet ended new sales of the 300D in August 2021, and vendor support is scheduled to end in August 2026 — effectively the end of its supported life. The current FortiADC catalog centers on the F-series (60F through 5000F), which succeeds the D-series with higher throughput and modern SSL acceleration. The 300D may still be available on the secondary/refurbished market for like-for-like replacement in existing 300D environments or budget-constrained lab use — new production deployments should strongly consider a current-generation F-series model instead. Confirm exact support status with Fortinet before purchasing.

300D at a Glance

Performance (Legacy)

Layer 4 throughput up to 6 Gbps and Layer 7 throughput up to 4 Gbps, with 725,000 Layer 7 requests per second — solid for its generation, but below current F-series equivalents.
Software-based SSL acceleration handles up to 1,500 SSL TPS at 2048-bit keys.

Load Balancing & SSL

Layer 4/7 load balancing with round robin, weighted round robin, least connections, and shortest-response methods, plus included Global Server Load Balancing and Link Load Balancing.
SSL offload throughput of 2.6 Gbps compression, with support for SSL forward proxy and HTTP/S mirroring for inspection use cases.

Connectivity

4x GE RJ45 plus 4x GE SFP network interfaces in a 1U rack-mount appliance, with 128 GB SSD onboard storage and support for up to 10 Virtual Domains.
Management via HTTPS, SSH CLI, direct console, and SNMP.

High Availability & Form Factor
