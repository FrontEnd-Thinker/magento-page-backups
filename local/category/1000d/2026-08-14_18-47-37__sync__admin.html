Fortinet 1000D. Email Security Gateway for Mid-to-Large Enterprises

The FortiMail 1000D is a 2U rack-mount appliance purpose-built for mid-to-large enterprises, education institutions, and government agencies with up to 3,000 users. With dual power supplies, hardware RAID across 2x 2TB drives (expandable), and up to 680,000 messages per hour of email routing throughput, it's built for demanding, high-volume messaging environments.
