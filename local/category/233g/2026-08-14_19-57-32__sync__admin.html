233G at a Glance

Wireless Performance

Tri-radio 802.11ax design: 2.4GHz, 5GHz, and the clean 6GHz Wi-Fi 6E band, each 2x2 MIMO.
Up to 574 Mbps (2.4GHz), 1201 Mbps (5GHz), and 2401 Mbps (6GHz) per-radio PHY rates.
OFDMA, UL/DL MU-MIMO, BSS Coloring, and Target Wake Time for dense-client efficiency.

Antennas & Coverage

Omnidirectional external antennas for the dual-band and tri-band/scanning elements, plus an internal BLE/ZigBee antenna.
Indoor AP; no IP ingress rating, not for outdoor or wet locations.
Ceiling, T-Rail, or wall mount with Kensington lock support.

Connectivity & Power

One 2.5GbE and one 1GbE RJ45 port for PoE diversity and uplink redundancy.
Powered by 802.3at PoE+, or Fortinet's SP-FAP400-PA power adapter.
UL2043 plenum-rated for above-ceiling installs.

Management & Security

Managed via an integrated FortiGate WLAN controller, FortiLAN Cloud, or FortiSASE cloud subscription.
Built-in WIDS/WIPS, spectrum analyzer, and packet sniffer mode.
Up to 24 SSIDs, WPA3 support, and 512 clients per radio.
