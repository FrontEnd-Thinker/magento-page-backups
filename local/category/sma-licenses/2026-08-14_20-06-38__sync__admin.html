SMA User Licenses at a Glance

Stackable Packs

Licenses are sold in 5-user and 100-user stackable packs compatible with the SMA 6210, 7210, and 8200v. A 5,000-user pack is available for the SMA 7210 and 8200v only. Purchase the count that matches your current peak usage and stack additional packs as your workforce grows, without replacing or re-provisioning the appliance.

Concurrent vs. Named Users

Concurrent user licenses count active sessions. If you have 500 licensed concurrent users, up to 500 people can be connected at the same time, but your total user roster can be much larger. Named user licenses are tied to specific accounts and are well suited for environments where a defined group of individuals needs access regardless of session count.

Pooled Option

Organizations running multiple SMA 1000 appliances under Central Management Server (CMS) can pool user licenses across all managed nodes. CMS allocates capacity dynamically based on real-time session demand, so a site that is quiet overnight can share its licensed capacity with a time zone that is at peak during those same hours.

Subscription Terms and Support

SMA User License subscriptions are available in 1, 2, and 3 year terms. All subscriptions include 24/7 technical support coverage. Longer terms lower the per-user annual cost. Licenses are managed through MySonicWall and apply immediately after registration.

Not sure which license count to order?

Start with your peak concurrent VPN session usage from your existing solution, add 20 to 30 percent for growth, and round up to the nearest pack size. Our team can help you work through the math and confirm the right SKU for your appliance model.
