Fortinet 421E. Indoor 4x4 MU-MIMO 802.11ac Wave 2 Access Point

The FortiAP 421E is a high-performance indoor access point built for dense enterprise environments — large offices, conference centers, and campus buildings. Its dual-radio, 4x4 MU-MIMO design supports 802.11ac Wave 2 with internal antennas for a clean ceiling or wall install. Note: the FortiAP 421E is an indoor-rated model with internal antennas — it is not an outdoor or weatherproof access point.
