T125-W at a Glance

Performance

UTM throughput (full scan with AV, IPS, App Control): 510 Mbps. Firewall throughput: 2.28 Gbps (UDP 1518). VPN throughput: 1.44 Gbps (UDP 1518). IPS throughput: 725 Mbps. HTTPS inspection: 270 Mbps. Antivirus: 880 Mbps. Concurrent connections: 3,850,000. New connections per second: 26,500.

Connectivity

1x 2.5Gb Ethernet WAN port and 4x 1Gb Ethernet LAN ports (5 wired ports total). Built-in Wi-Fi 7 (802.11be) with dual-band external SMA antennas for greater throughput and range than internal antenna designs. 1 Serial port and 2 USB-A 3.2 ports. 5 EDR Core sensors included. Unrestricted VLANs for network segmentation.

VPN and SD-WAN

10 Branch Office VPN (site-to-site) tunnels. 10 Mobile VPN connections. Site-to-site VPN over IKEv2, IPSec, policy and route-based tunnels, and TLS hub and spoke. Remote access over IKEv2, IPSec, L2TP, and TLS. SD-WAN with multi-WAN failover, dynamic path selection, and jitter, loss, and latency measurement. Active/passive and active/active high availability clustering.

Security Services

Stateful packet inspection, TLS decryption, and proxy firewall. Basic Security Suite includes IPS, Gateway AV, Application Control, WebBlocker, spamBlocker, and Reputation Enabled Defense. Total Security Suite adds APT Blocker, IntelligentAV, DNSWatch, ThreatSync (XDR), and EDR Core. Over 100 dashboards and reports in WatchGuard Cloud, including PCI and HIPAA compliance.

T125-W FAQ

The T125-W integrates the firewall and wireless radio under a single WatchGuard Cloud policy and management console. You avoid paying a separate AP license, and there is no second device to deploy, configure, or renew. For small offices where one or two coverage zones is enough, the T125-W is cleaner and less expensive than pairing a wired T125 with a standalone AP.

The T125-W uses Wi-Fi 7 (802.11be) with dual-band external SMA antennas. Wi-Fi 7 supports higher aggregate throughput and lower latency compared to Wi-Fi 6 and 6E, and the external antennas give you more flexibility in placing the appliance for better coverage than internal antenna designs would allow in a compact tabletop unit.

WatchGuard rates the T125-W for up to 40 users, counting both wired and wireless clients. If your site grows past 40 users or you need separate coverage zones, consider pairing a T145-W with dedicated WatchGuard AP hardware managed through the same WatchGuard Cloud account, giving you more throughput capacity and more wireless coverage flexibility.

Yes. Site-to-site VPN over IKEv2, IPSec, and TLS, plus mobile VPN over IKEv2, IPSec, L2TP, and TLS are all supported, identical to the wired T125. The T125-W supports 10 Branch Office VPN tunnels and 10 Mobile VPN connections. Adding wireless to the appliance does not reduce any of the VPN or firewall capabilities.

Yes. The T125-W supports unrestricted VLANs, letting you put wireless clients on a separate guest or IoT VLAN from wired corporate traffic. Each VLAN gets its own policy and firewall rules, so a device on the guest wireless network cannot reach wired corporate resources without explicit permission in your ruleset.
