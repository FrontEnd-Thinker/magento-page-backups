Panda VPN at a Glance

Traffic Encryption

Encrypts all internet traffic leaving your device using strong VPN protocols. On public Wi-Fi networks at airports, hotels, cafes, and other shared locations, encrypted traffic cannot be intercepted or read by other users on the same network or by anyone monitoring the connection.

IP Address Masking

Routes your traffic through Panda's VPN servers, replacing your real IP address with the server's IP address. Websites, advertisers, and analytics services see the VPN server's location instead of yours, reducing the amount of personal data tied to your browsing sessions.

No-Log Privacy Policy

Panda VPN operates a no-activity-log policy, meaning that browsing activity and connection logs are not stored on the VPN servers. Users connect and browse knowing that their session data is not being collected or stored in a way that could be accessed or shared with third parties.

Simple One-Click Connection

Connecting to Panda VPN requires a single button press in the application. The service automatically selects the fastest available server for your location. Users who want to choose a specific country or server can do so from the server selection menu without any manual configuration of network settings.

All VPNs introduce some latency because your traffic is encrypted and routed through an additional server before reaching its destination.
