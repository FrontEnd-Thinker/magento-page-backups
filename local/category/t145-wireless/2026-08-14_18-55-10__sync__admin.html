T145-W at a Glance

Performance

UTM throughput (full scan with AV, IPS, App Control): 710 Mbps. Firewall throughput: 3.90 Gbps (UDP 1518). VPN throughput: 1.94 Gbps (UDP 1518). IPS throughput: 1.03 Gbps. HTTPS inspection: 380 Mbps. Antivirus: 1.06 Gbps. Concurrent connections: 3,850,000. New connections per second: 26,500. Adding wireless does not reduce any throughput spec versus the wired T145.

Connectivity

1x 2.5Gb Ethernet, 4x 1Gb Ethernet, and 1x SFP/SFP+ (accepts 1Gb SFP or 10Gb SFP+ transceivers). Built-in Wi-Fi 7 (802.11be) with dual-band external SMA antennas for greater range and throughput. Fully fanless and silent operation. 20 EDR Core sensors included. 1 Serial port and 2 USB-A 3.2 ports. Unrestricted VLANs.

VPN and SD-WAN

25 Branch Office VPN (site-to-site) tunnels. 25 Mobile VPN connections. Site-to-site VPN over IKEv2, IPSec, policy and route-based tunnels, and TLS hub and spoke. Remote access over IKEv2, IPSec, L2TP, and TLS. SD-WAN with multi-WAN failover, dynamic path selection, and jitter, loss, and latency measurement. Active/passive and active/active high availability clustering.

Security Services

Stateful packet inspection, TLS decryption, and proxy firewall. Basic Security Suite includes IPS, Gateway AV, Application Control, WebBlocker, spamBlocker, and Reputation Enabled Defense. Total Security Suite adds APT Blocker, IntelligentAV, DNSWatch, ThreatSync (XDR), and 20 EDR Core sensors. Over 100 dashboards and reports in WatchGuard Cloud, including PCI and HIPAA compliance.

T145-W FAQ

The T145-W is identical to the T145 in every performance and port spec, including 710 Mbps UTM throughput, the SFP/SFP+ fiber port, and 25 Branch Office VPN tunnels. The only difference is the built-in Wi-Fi 7 radio with external dual-band antennas on the T145-W. If your site needs integrated wireless and you do not want to buy and manage a separate access point, the T145-W is the straightforward choice.

The T145-W uses Wi-Fi 7 (802.11be) with external SMA antennas, supporting higher aggregate throughput and lower latency than Wi-Fi 6 and 6E connections. The external antennas let you orient them for better coverage in your space. For a single open-plan office with up to 75 users, the built-in radio provides solid coverage. Larger or multi-floor sites should add dedicated WatchGuard AP hardware managed through the same WatchGuard Cloud account.

Yes. Like the wired T145, the T145-W is built around an ultra-low-power CPU that allows fully fanless operation at all load levels. It runs silently, making it a good fit for open-plan offices, reception areas, and quiet environments where fan noise would be disruptive. Adding the Wi-Fi 7 radio does not change the fanless design of the appliance.

The SFP/SFP+ port accepts 1Gb SFP or 10Gb SFP+ transceivers for a high-speed fiber WAN uplink or a connection to a core switch. This lets the T145-W handle multi-gig fiber ISP circuits without the copper WAN port creating a throughput bottleneck. Transceivers are sold separately and must be compatible with WatchGuard hardware.

Yes. The T145-W supports active/passive and active/active HA clustering in the same way as the wired T145. Two T145-W units can cluster together for failover or load sharing. HA requires WatchGuard's HA license, and both units in the cluster must be the same model. Both wired and wireless interfaces participate in the cluster configuration.
