Fortinet VM01. Virtual Email Security Gateway — 1 vCPU

FortiMail VM01 is the entry-level virtual appliance license in Fortinet's FortiMail lineup, licensed for a single vCPU. It's built for small businesses and branch offices that want enterprise-grade anti-spam, anti-malware, and threat protection running inside their own VMware, Hyper-V, KVM, Citrix XenServer, or cloud environment — no dedicated appliance required. VM01 supports up to 20 protected email domains and roughly 150 server-mode mailboxes, making it right-sized for small organizations. It shares the same hypervisor and public-cloud support as the rest of the FortiMail VM family, so you can step up to VM02 or higher as mail volume grows.

VM01 FAQ

VM01 is licensed for 1 vCPU maximum — Fortinet's smallest production FortiMail VM tier, sized for small businesses and branch offices.

FortiMail VM01 needs a minimum of 2 GB RAM (up to 4 GB) and at least 250 GB of disk for initial deployment, with up to 1 TB supported.

VM01 runs on VMware ESX/ESXi, Microsoft Hyper-V, Citrix XenServer, and KVM, as well as AWS, Microsoft Azure, Google Cloud Platform, and Oracle Cloud Infrastructure.

VM02 doubles the licensed vCPU count to 2, raises maximum RAM to 8 GB, doubles maximum storage to 2 TB, and adds Data Loss Prevention and optional Microsoft 365/Google Workspace API integration, which VM01 doesn't include.
