T115-W at a Glance

Performance

UTM throughput (full scan with AV, IPS, App Control): 280 Mbps. Firewall throughput: 1.02 Gbps (UDP 1518). VPN throughput: 640 Mbps (UDP 1518). IPS throughput: 375 Mbps. HTTPS inspection: 140 Mbps. Antivirus: 450 Mbps. Concurrent connections: 3,850,000. New connections per second: 26,500.

Connectivity

3x 1Gb Ethernet ports. Built-in Wi-Fi 7 (802.11be) with external antennas for dual-band wireless. Silent, fully fanless design with ultra-quiet operation. 4GB of RAM reduces throughput bottlenecks at higher bandwidths. 1 Serial port and 1 USB-A 3.2 port. Compact form factor for desk or cabinet mounting.

VPN and SD-WAN

5 Branch Office VPN (site-to-site) tunnels. 5 Mobile VPN connections. Site-to-site VPN over IKEv2, IPSec, policy and route-based tunnels, and TLS hub and spoke. Remote access VPN over IKEv2, IPSec, L2TP, and TLS. SD-WAN with multi-WAN failover and dynamic path selection. Jitter, loss, and latency measurement.

Security Services

Stateful packet inspection, TLS decryption, and proxy firewall. IPS, Gateway AV, Application Control, WebBlocker, spamBlocker, and Reputation Enabled Defense included with Basic Security Suite. Total Security Suite adds APT Blocker, DNSWatch, ThreatSync (XDR), and EDR Core. Note: Access Portal and IntelligentAV are not available on the T115-W.
