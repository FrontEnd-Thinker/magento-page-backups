Fortinet 221E. Indoor 802.11ac Wave 2 Access Point

The FortiAP 221E is a compact indoor wireless access point built for everyday office and classroom Wi-Fi. Its dual-radio 802.11ac Wave 2 design with 4 internal omni-directional antennas delivers reliable coverage for standard-density spaces. Centrally managed from a FortiGate or FortiLAN Cloud, it's an easy, secure entry point into enterprise-grade Wi-Fi.

221E FAQ

The FortiAP 221E supports 802.11ac Wave 2 with dual-band 2x2 MU-MIMO, delivering up to 1,167 Mbps of aggregate throughput.

It uses 4 internal omni-directional antennas, making it ideal for standard indoor coverage in offices and classrooms rather than high-density or directional deployments.

Yes, it's powered by 802.3af PoE from a FortiGate switch port or a compatible PoE injector.

Yes, it can be centrally managed and secured through FortiGate (via FortiLink) or through FortiLAN Cloud for cloud-based management.

The 221E is an indoor AP with internal omni antennas, while the 222E and 224E are outdoor, IP67-rated models, and the 223E swaps in external antenna connectors for directional/sector antenna options in high-density indoor deployments.
