Switch 14-24 at a Glance

Performance

128 Gbps non-blocking switching capacity with a 32K MAC address table and 1.5M packet buffer. Four 10G SFP+ uplinks deliver 10x the uplink speed of 1G-only switches, keeping your network core from becoming a bottleneck.

Connectivity

24 x 1G RJ45 copper ports for wired clients and servers, plus 4 x 10G SFP+ ports for high-speed uplinks or server connections. Supports 802.3ad link aggregation for redundant uplinks and 4,096 VLANs for network segmentation.

Management

Managed through SonicWall firewalls or Wireless Network Manager with centralized VLAN configuration, switch auto-discovery, and daisy chaining. Also configurable via standalone web GUI, CLI, SSH, and SNMP. Supports zero-touch deployment via Capture Security Center.

Power and Form Factor

25W fixed power supply (100-240 VAC). Maximum power consumption: 24.4W. Fanless 1U rackmount design: 440 x 200 x 44mm, 1.8 kg. Exceptionally reliable with an MTBF rated at over 1.27 million hours (145 years).

Switch 14-24 FAQ

The SonicWall Switch 14-24 (model SWS14-24) is a 1U rackmount Layer 2 managed gigabit switch with 24 x 1G RJ45 copper ports and 4 x 10G SFP+ uplink ports. It delivers 128 Gbps of switching capacity and is designed for wiring closet deployments in medium-sized businesses and distributed branch offices. It does not include Power over Ethernet and runs passively cooled without fans.

The Switch 14-24 supports SonicWall-approved SFP and SFP+ modules. Common options include 1000Base-SX for short-range multi-mode fiber, 1000Base-LX for single-mode fiber, and 10G SFP+ modules for 10 gigabit copper or fiber connections. Refer to SonicWall's supported SFP and SFP+ module list to confirm compatibility before purchasing optics.

Yes. The Switch 14-24 includes a standalone web GUI and CLI for full configuration without a SonicWall firewall. It supports SNMP monitoring, SSH management, VLAN configuration, QoS, 802.1X port security, and link aggregation in standalone mode. Connecting it to a SonicWall firewall adds centralized management, zero-touch deployment, switch auto-discovery, and unified security policy enforcement.

The Switch 14-24 supports up to 4,096 VLANs using IEEE 802.1Q tagging. You can create access ports for end-user devices, trunk ports for uplinks carrying multiple VLANs, voice VLANs for IP phones, and guest VLANs for isolated visitor access. When managed through a SonicWall firewall, VLAN policies are pushed centrally, and the firewall enforces inter-VLAN routing and security rules.

The Switch 14-24 has 24 x 1G copper ports and 128 Gbps switching capacity, while the Switch 14-48 has 48 x 1G copper ports and 176 Gbps switching capacity. Both include 4 x 10G SFP+ uplinks and lack PoE. The Switch 14-48 is the right choice when you need more port density in the same 1U rack space. PoE versions of both are available as the Switch 14-24-FPoE and Switch 14-48-FPoE.
