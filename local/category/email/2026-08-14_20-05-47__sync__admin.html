Email Security at a Glance

Threat Protection

Blocks spam, malware, and phishing on inbound and outbound mail, backed by live SophosLabs threat intelligence. Advanced adds Active Threat Protection: cloud sandboxing that detonates suspicious attachments before they execute, plus Time-of-Click URL rewriting that re-checks links the moment a user clicks them.

Impersonation & BEC Defense

Compares sender display names against your VIPs and commonly spoofed brands, flags look-alike domains, and can block, quarantine, or banner suspect messages. It also runs post-delivery protection for Microsoft 365, pulling phishing emails back out of the inbox if a link turns malicious after it is delivered.

Encryption & DLP

Scans message bodies and attachments for financials, PII, and health data, then applies policy-based encryption or blocks the send. Encrypt with TLS, S/MIME, or push-based delivery, with an optional web portal add-on for full pull-based encryption.

Deployment & Management

Connect with a standard MX record change, or on Microsoft 365 with Advanced, use Sophos Mailflow Rules for API-based setup with no MX redirect. Sync users with AD or Azure AD, set domain, group, or per-user policies, and manage it all in Sophos Central.
