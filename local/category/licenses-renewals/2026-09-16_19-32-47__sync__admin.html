SonicWall License & Renewal FAQ

Log in to your MySonicWall account at mysonicwall.com and navigate to My Products. Each registered appliance shows the expiration date for every active subscription. You can also check from the firewall itself by opening the SonicOS management interface and going to the Licenses page under Device settings. If you see an expiration within 90 days, now is the best time to renew because there is no gap in coverage.

When your security services subscription lapses, the firewall continues to pass traffic but stops receiving signature updates for anti-virus, anti-spyware, IPS, and application control. Content filtering and Capture ATP sandboxing also stop working. Your network is still online but no longer protected against new threats. Firmware updates and technical support access also end. You can renew at any time to restore all services. The renewal term starts from your original expiration date, so you do not lose any time you already paid for.

EPSS (Essential Protection Service Suite) covers the core security stack: Capture ATP sandboxing, Gateway Anti-Virus, Anti-Spyware, IPS, Application Control, Content Filtering, Anti-Spam, and 24x7 support. APSS (Advanced Protection Service Suite) includes everything in EPSS and adds DNS Security, Cloud App Security (CAS), and NSM Essential cloud management. APSS is the better choice if you need centralized cloud management or want DNS layer protection to block threats before they reach your firewall.

Yes. You can upgrade from EPSS to APSS at any point during your subscription. Contact our sales team with your serial number and current expiration date. We will quote the difference so you can upgrade without losing any remaining time on your existing EPSS subscription.

If you own a Gen 5, Gen 6, or older Gen 7 appliance, the Secure Upgrade Plus (SUP) program may save you money. SUP lets you trade in your current firewall for a new Gen 8 unit bundled with a multi-year security suite at a steep discount. In many cases the total cost lands within $50 to $150 of a straight license renewal, so you get brand new hardware for roughly the same investment. Our sales team can quote both options side by side so you can compare total cost of ownership.

SonicWall license keys are digital products. After your order is processed, the license key is emailed directly from SonicWall to the address on your MySonicWall account. Most keys are delivered within one business hour during business hours (Monday through Friday, 9am to 6pm EST). Once you have the key, log in to MySonicWall, apply it to your appliance, and the firewall pulls the activation automatically.
