Fortinet 423E. Indoor 4x4 MU-MIMO Access Point with External Antennas

The FortiAP 423E delivers the same high-performance, dual-radio 4x4 MU-MIMO 802.11ac Wave 2 platform as the 421E, but with external antenna connectors for custom coverage patterns and directional antenna options. It's a strong fit for large indoor spaces — warehouses, gyms, and auditoriums — where fine-tuned antenna placement matters. Note: the FortiAP 423E is an indoor-rated model, not an outdoor or weatherproof access point.
