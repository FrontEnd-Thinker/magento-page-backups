Full Encryption at a Glance

Policy Enforcement

Define encryption requirements by device group and enforce them through Aether policies. Devices that do not meet the encryption policy are flagged for remediation. Administrators can enable or disable BitLocker and FileVault remotely without touching the endpoint.

Secure Key Escrow

Recovery keys for BitLocker and FileVault are automatically escrowed in the Aether cloud console. When a user forgets their PIN or a device needs to be accessed during an incident, IT administrators can retrieve the correct recovery key in seconds without contacting the user or physically accessing the device.

Compliance Reporting

Generate reports that show encryption status, policy compliance rate, and key escrow confirmation for every device in the fleet. These reports satisfy the data-at-rest encryption documentation requirements of PCI DSS, HIPAA, GDPR, and other frameworks that mandate encryption of sensitive information on portable devices.

No Additional Encryption Software

Panda Full Encryption manages the native encryption tools built into Windows and macOS, rather than introducing a third-party encryption layer. This avoids compatibility issues, reduces agent footprint, and works with the hardware-accelerated encryption capabilities already present in modern devices.
