T125 at a Glance

Performance

UTM throughput (full scan with AV, IPS, App Control): 510 Mbps. Firewall throughput: 2.28 Gbps (UDP 1518). VPN throughput: 1.44 Gbps (UDP 1518). IPS throughput: 725 Mbps. HTTPS inspection: 270 Mbps. Antivirus: 880 Mbps. Concurrent connections: 3,850,000. New connections per second: 26,500.

Connectivity

1x 2.5Gb Ethernet WAN port and 4x 1Gb Ethernet LAN ports (5 total). 1 Serial port and 2 USB-A 3.2 ports. Unrestricted VLAN support for network segmentation. 5 EDR Core sensors included. Dynamic routing via RIP, OSPF, and BGP. Link aggregation with 802.3ad dynamic, static, and active/backup modes.

VPN and SD-WAN

10 Branch Office VPN (site-to-site) tunnels. 10 Mobile VPN connections. Site-to-site VPN over IKEv2, IPSec, policy and route-based tunnels, and TLS hub and spoke. Remote access over IKEv2, IPSec, L2TP, and TLS. SD-WAN with multi-WAN failover, dynamic path selection, and jitter, loss, and latency measurement. Active/passive and active/active HA clustering.

Security Services

Stateful packet inspection, TLS decryption, and proxy firewall. Basic Security Suite includes IPS, Gateway AV, Application Control, WebBlocker, spamBlocker, and Reputation Enabled Defense. Total Security Suite adds APT Blocker, IntelligentAV, DNSWatch, ThreatSync (XDR), and EDR Core. Over 100 dashboards and reports available in WatchGuard Cloud, including PCI and HIPAA compliance reports.

T125 FAQ

The T125 is the wired-only model with five Ethernet ports and a 2.5Gb WAN port. The T125-W adds built-in Wi-Fi 7 with external dual-band antennas. Both share identical throughput specs (510 Mbps UTM, 2.28 Gbps firewall) and support 10 Branch Office VPN tunnels. If your site needs wireless, the T125-W keeps everything under one appliance and one WatchGuard Cloud policy.

WatchGuard sizes the T125 for up to 40 users. Actual capacity depends on your ISP speed, which security services you run, and your traffic mix. Offices that are approaching 40 users, have a faster ISP circuit, or run deep inspection on all HTTPS traffic may want to look at the T145, which delivers 710 Mbps UTM throughput and supports up to 75 users.
