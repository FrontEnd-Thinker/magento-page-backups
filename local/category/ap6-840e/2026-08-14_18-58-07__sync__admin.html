AP6 840E at a Glance

Wi-Fi 6/6E Performance

Tri-radio operation across the 2.4, 5, and less-congested 6 GHz band (where available), with 4x4:4 MIMO for maximum throughput in high-density spaces. WPA3 security is built in, and DFS is supported.

Wiring & Power

Two 2.5GbE interfaces keep the LAN from becoming the bottleneck. The 840E is the only AP6 model that needs 802.3bt PoE++ (up to 45W) to run all three radios at once. Power it with the Sophos CS210-8FP or CS1010-8FP switch, or the Sophos PoE++ 60W injector.

Deployment

Built for indoor use with desktop, wall, or ceiling mounting. Plenum-rated (UL2043), so it's approved for the air-handling space above drop ceilings. Measures 8.86 x 8.86 x 1.54 in and weighs 1.2 kg.

Management & Warranty

Managed entirely in Sophos Central, with a local web interface available for a single AP. Backed by a limited lifetime hardware warranty; an active support subscription adds Central management, firmware updates, Advanced RMA, and 24/7 support.
