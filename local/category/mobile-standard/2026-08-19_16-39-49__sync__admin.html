Mobile Standard at a Glance

What Standard Covers

Device and app management. Enroll devices, push apps, apply configuration profiles, and enforce policy across corporate-owned and BYOD endpoints. Configure Microsoft Office 365 apps on iOS and Android, and set up email, Wi-Fi, and VPN from a single place.

Platforms & Enrollment

Manage iPhones, iPads, Android phones and tablets, Chromebooks, Windows 10 computers, and Macs. Roll out at scale with Android zero-touch and Apple DEP, or keep personal data private with the Android Enterprise Work Profile and iOS User Enrollment.

Managed from Sophos Central

Everything runs in Sophos Central, the same cloud console as your endpoint, server, and network security. No on-prem server to install, and a self-service portal lets users enroll their own devices. You can be up and running in minutes.

Standard vs Advanced

Standard is management first. Sophos Mobile Advanced includes everything in Standard plus Intercept X for Mobile threat defense and Android container features such as Secure Workspace and Secure Email. Step up when you want on-device protection too.

Standard or Advanced?

If you also want on-device threat defense with Intercept X for Mobile, step up to Sophos Mobile Advanced. Compare what each tier covers before you buy so you only pay for what your fleet needs.
