Rack Mount Kits at a Glance

Compatibility

Barracuda ships a dedicated kit per chassis size rather than one universal tray — for example BNGF18B.RK015 for the F18B and BNGF280C.RK020 for the F280C. Tell us your exact model and we'll confirm the matching part number.

What's Included

A pair of L-shaped metal brackets sized to your appliance's chassis, plus the screws needed to attach them to the unit and to your rack's front posts.

Installation

Brackets bolt to the sides of the appliance, then screw directly into standard rack rails. No drilling, no cage nuts required on most 19" racks — just a screwdriver.

Rack Space Planning

Each kit occupies 1U. Barracuda doesn't sell a shared, side-by-side dual-unit tray for the F-Series desktop line, so plan on one rack slot per appliance.

Not sure which CloudGen Firewall you have?

Rack mount kits are matched to the exact F-Series chassis size, so getting the model right matters. Compare the F18 through F280 lineup to confirm which unit — and which kit — you need.
