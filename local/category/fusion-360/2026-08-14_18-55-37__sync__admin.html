Fusion 360 at a Glance

Full AD360 Security Stack

Includes everything in Adaptive Defense 360: next-gen antivirus, behavioral EDR, Zero-Trust Application Service classifying 100% of running processes, exploit prevention, and Panda's 24/7 threat hunting service.

Complete RMM Capabilities

Includes the full Panda Systems Management platform: remote desktop, script deployment, patch management for OS and third-party apps, software inventory, hardware asset tracking, and automated maintenance scheduling.

Single Agent, Single Console

One lightweight agent delivers both security and IT management functions. IT and security teams share the Aether console, eliminating the overhead of managing separate platforms, reconciling separate device inventories, and paying for redundant infrastructure.

MSP-Ready Architecture

Multi-tenant support in Aether keeps client environments isolated while allowing MSPs to manage all accounts from a single login. Fusion 360 is a popular choice for MSPs looking to deliver a bundled security and IT management service under one per-seat price.
