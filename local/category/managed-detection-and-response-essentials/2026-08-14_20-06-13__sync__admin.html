MDR Essentials at a Glance

What You Get

A live security team, not another dashboard. The Sophos SOC handles 24/7 threat monitoring, investigation, and response actions, backed by expert-led threat hunting, active threat containment to stop an attack from spreading, and root cause analysis on confirmed cases.

Response and Containment

They stop the bleeding, then guide the cleanup. Under Essentials, Sophos contains a confirmed threat and hands you clear steps to neutralize it. A deep, full-scale incident response is a separate engagement at this tier, or it comes included when you step up to MDR Complete with no hourly caps.

Works With Your Stack

Keep what you already run. Essentials supports every Sophos product plus 350+ third-party integrations across firewall, cloud, email, identity, and network. Microsoft 365 audit logs and the Graph Security API are supported at no extra cost. Integration pack licenses apply to non-Sophos sources.

Requirements and Retention

Light to deploy. Managed endpoints run Sophos XDR or the lightweight XDR Sensor agent. Standard security data storage is 90 days, with an optional one year extended retention license when compliance calls for a longer window.
