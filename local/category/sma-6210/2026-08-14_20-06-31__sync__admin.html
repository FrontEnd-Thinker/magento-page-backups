SMA 6210 at a Glance

Performance

Up to 1.3 Gbps SSL VPN throughput with AES-NI hardware acceleration. Dual 1TB SATA drives configured in RAID 1 protect configuration and log data. A TPM security chip guards appliance integrity at the hardware level.

Connectivity

Six 1GbE copper ports plus a dedicated out-of-band management port. Active/Active and Active/Passive high availability keep access online through hardware failures and planned maintenance windows. Fixed power supply rated at 77W.

Access Security

FIPS 140-2 Level 2 certified with optional anti-tamper protection. Advanced Endpoint Control (EPC) checks device health, OS patch level, and certificate status before granting access. Role-based policies restrict each user to only the resources they need.

Management

Manageable standalone via browser-based console or centrally through the SonicWall Central Management Server (CMS) for multi-site deployments. Integrates with RADIUS, LDAP, Active Directory, and SAML 2.0 identity providers. MFA enforced per policy.
