AP6 840 at a Glance

Wi-Fi 6 Performance

Dual-band 2.4 and 5 GHz radios in a 4x4:4 design, built to keep up in high-density rooms. WPA3 security and DFS channel support come standard, so more devices stay fast and protected.

Connectivity & Power

A single 2.5GbE uplink clears the gigabit bottleneck. It runs on 802.3at PoE+ (30W max), and the plain SKU ships without an injector, so pair it with a Sophos PoE+ injector or a PoE-capable switch.

Cloud Management

Manage your full Wi-Fi estate in Sophos Central, right next to your firewalls and switches. A local web interface is available for setting up a single AP6 on site.

Build & Deployment

Indoor desktop, wall, or ceiling mount, and plenum-rated (UL2043) for in-ceiling installs. Backed by a limited lifetime hardware warranty.
