Fortinet 222E. Outdoor 802.11ac Wave 2 Access Point

The FortiAP 222E is a ruggedized, IP67-rated outdoor access point designed to extend enterprise Wi-Fi to parking lots, courtyards, and other outdoor areas. Its external, high-gain omni-directional antennas and dual-radio 802.11ac Wave 2 design deliver strong coverage in tough environmental conditions. It's centrally managed alongside your indoor FortiAPs from a FortiGate or FortiLAN Cloud.
