NSa 5700 at a Glance

Performance

Firewall inspection throughput of 19 Gbps with full threat prevention active at 11 Gbps. VPN throughput of 15 Gbps and a maximum of 10 Million concurrent connections handle peak enterprise workloads without performance degradation.

Interfaces

16x 1GbE, 8x 10GbE SFP+, 4x 25GbE SFP28, 2U form factor. Multiple interface speeds and SFP options support flexible uplink configurations for both copper and fiber deployments.

Security Services

Advanced Gateway Security Suite (AGSS) includes Gateway Anti-Virus, IPS, Application Control, Content Filtering, and Capture ATP cloud sandboxing. TLS 1.3 decryption and inspection runs inline without bypassing the security stack.

Management

SonicOS 7.0 with REST API and centralized management via NSM. Zero-touch provisioning, role-based access control, and SIEM integration via syslog support enterprise-scale deployment and operations.

NSa 5700 FAQ

The NSa 5700 is a Gen 7 next-generation firewall designed for large enterprise cores and data center edge deployments. It provides 19 Gbps of firewall inspection throughput and 11 Gbps of full threat prevention for networks that cannot afford performance trade-offs when security services are active.

The NSa 5700 is available as a standalone appliance or bundled with the Essential Protection Service Suite (EPSS) or Advanced Protection Service Suite (APSS).
