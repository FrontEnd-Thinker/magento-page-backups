TZ80 at a Glance

Performance

750 Mbps firewall inspection throughput and 750 Mbps threat prevention throughput. Supports up to 300,000 concurrent SPI connections and 50 site-to-site VPN tunnels.

Interfaces

Four 1GbE copper ports, one 1G SFP uplink, USB Type-A for cellular failover dongles, and USB Type-C for out-of-band management and console access.

VPN and SD-WAN

Up to 50 site-to-site IPSec VPN tunnels, SSL VPN for remote workers, and Secure SD-WAN capability for intelligent multi-WAN link management without added licensing cost.

Security Services

SonicWall Deep Packet Inspection (DPI), Gateway Anti-Virus, Anti-Spyware, IPS, Content Filtering Service 5.0, Capture ATP with RTDMI cloud sandbox, and DNS Security.
