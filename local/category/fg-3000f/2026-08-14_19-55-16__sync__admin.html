FortiGate 3000F at a Glance

Performance

397 Gbps IPv4 firewall throughput (1518-byte UDP). 36 Gbps IPS throughput. 34 Gbps NGFW throughput. 33 Gbps threat protection throughput. All throughput figures are measured with logging enabled on an enterprise traffic mix, so the numbers reflect real inspection workloads, not synthetic best-case runs.

Connectivity

6x 100GE QSFP28 / 40GE QSFP+ slots, each breakout-capable into 4x 25GE SFP28. 16x 25GE SFP28 / 10GE SFP+ / GE SFP data ports including 2 dedicated HA slots. 16x multi-speed 10G/5G/2.5G/GE RJ45 data ports. 2x 10GE/GE RJ45 management ports on a separate management path, isolated from the data plane.

VPN Capacity

105 Gbps IPsec VPN throughput (AES256-SHA256, hardware-accelerated). 11 Gbps SSL VPN throughput. 40,000 gateway-to-gateway IPsec tunnels. 200,000 client-to-gateway IPsec tunnels. SSL VPN supports up to 30,000 concurrent remote users, making this series well-suited to large distributed workforces and SD-WAN overlay architectures.

Capacity

70 million concurrent sessions standard, expandable to 230 million with a Hyperscale Firewall License. 500 virtual domains (VDOMs) for multi-tenant segmentation. Dual NP7 Network Processors and dual CP9 Content Processors provide hardware-accelerated VXLAN, DDoS protection, and elephant flow handling without burdening the main CPU.

FortiGate 3000F FAQ

The FG-3000F and FG-3001F run on identical hardware and deliver the same throughput, port density, and processor complement. The only hardware difference is onboard storage: the 3001F ships with 2x 1 TB SSDs (2 TB usable) for local log retention, which removes the need for a dedicated FortiAnalyzer in smaller deployments. The -DC suffix on either model indicates a direct-current power supply for data center environments that distribute DC power.

Fortinet publishes 33 Gbps for threat protection throughput, which measures the appliance running IPS, application control, and malware protection simultaneously on an enterprise traffic mix with logging enabled. That is the number to plan around if you intend to run full inspection. The 397 Gbps firewall throughput figure applies to a stateful firewall-only workload on large packet sizes, which is a different and lighter workload than full NGFW.

Most commonly at the enterprise perimeter edge, at the data center core as a segmentation device between internal zones, and in hybrid IT environments that need 100GE uplinks to the network core. Service providers use the multi-tenant VDOM architecture to manage up to 500 isolated policy domains on a single chassis. The -DC models serve telecom and carrier environments where DC power distribution is standard. Fortinet recommends this series for organizations running ISP connections up to 11 Gbps and expecting 500 to 2,000 concurrent users on the NGFW.

Full NGFW capability requires a FortiGuard bundle that activates IPS, application control, and URL filtering at a minimum. Fortinet sells two main bundles for this series: the Unified Threat Protection (UTP) bundle, which covers IPS, antivirus, application control, URL filtering, and antispam, and the Enterprise Protection bundle, which adds AI-based inline malware prevention, DNS filtering, inline CASB, data loss prevention, attack surface security, and the FortiConverter migration service. Both require a FortiCare support contract, which covers firmware access, technical support, and hardware warranty.

Redundant, hot-swap AC power supplies ship standard on both AC models, and the dual power design covers the unit even during a supply failure without a planned maintenance window.
