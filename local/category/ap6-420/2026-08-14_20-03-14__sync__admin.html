AP6 420 at a Glance

Wi-Fi 6 Performance

Dual-radio 802.11ax on the 2.4 and 5 GHz bands with a 2x2:2 MU-MIMO design. Wi-Fi 6 brings better airtime efficiency and battery-friendly TWT scheduling, so a room full of laptops, phones, and IoT devices behaves in high-density spaces.

Connectivity

One 1GbE port with PoE+ (802.3at) for both power and data over a single cable. The 420 is the value model in the AP6 lineup; step up to the 840 or any "E" model if you need a 2.5G uplink.

Deployment

Indoor, with desktop, wall, or ceiling mounting. Internal omnidirectional antennas and a low-profile housing suit offices, retail, clinics, and classrooms. Plenum and mount kits are available separately.

Management & Warranty

Managed exclusively in Sophos Central, with a local web UI for single-AP setup. Backed by a limited lifetime warranty; a Sophos Central support subscription per AP unlocks firmware updates, 24/7 support, and Advanced RMA.
