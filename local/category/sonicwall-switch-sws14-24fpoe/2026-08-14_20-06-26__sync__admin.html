Switch 14-24-FPoE at a Glance

Full PoE+ on Every Port

All 24 copper ports support 802.3af/at PoE+ with up to 30W per port and a 410W total PoE budget. Time Management Options allow scheduled power delivery per port to reduce energy consumption outside of business hours.

Performance

128 Gbps non-blocking switching capacity with a 32K MAC table and 1.5M packet buffer. Four 10G SFP+ uplinks provide high-speed connectivity to your core switch or SonicWall firewall, keeping inter-VLAN and internet traffic fast.

Management

Managed through SonicWall firewalls or Wireless Network Manager for centralized VLAN configuration, per-port PoE control, switch software upgrades, and zero-touch deployment. Also supports standalone web GUI, CLI, SSH, and SNMP monitoring.

Power and Form Factor

480W fixed power supply (100-240 VAC). Maximum power consumption: 500.4W. Two cooling fans. 1U rackmount design: 440 x 260 x 44mm, 3.9 kg. MTBF rated at over 157,000 hours. Includes a serial console port for out-of-band management.

Switch 14-24-FPoE FAQ

The Switch 14-24-FPoE has 24 PoE+ ports with a total budget of 410W. How many devices you can run simultaneously depends on each device's power draw. For example, 16 access points drawing 25W each would consume 400W, comfortably within budget. A mix of 20 VoIP phones at 6W and 6 access points at 25W would total 270W, leaving ample headroom. The switch manages power distribution automatically and supports priority-based power feeding to protect critical devices first.

Yes. The Switch 14-24-FPoE is an ideal companion for SonicWave access points. The 802.3at PoE+ standard and 30W per-port capacity cover the power requirements of SonicWave 231, 621, and 641 series APs. When both the switch and the access points are managed through a SonicWall firewall or Wireless Network Manager, you get unified visibility, VLAN-based wireless segmentation, and centralized policy management from a single interface.

The Switch 14-24-FPoE requires a minimum 10-amp power circuit. With a 480W power supply and maximum consumption of 500.4W (including full PoE load), it is important to ensure the circuit and power strip or PDU in your rack can support the load. In high-density PoE deployments, verify your rack power budget before installation.

Yes. The Switch 14-24-FPoE supports IEEE 802.3ad Link Aggregation Control Protocol (LACP) and static link aggregation (port trunking). You can bond two or more of the 10G SFP+ uplinks into a single logical link for increased bandwidth and uplink redundancy. If one physical uplink fails, traffic automatically flows through the remaining links without interruption.

The Switch 14-24 is a non-PoE model with a 25W power supply that draws only 24.4W, making it a lightweight, passively cooled choice for environments where all devices have their own power supplies. The Switch 14-24-FPoE adds 802.3at PoE+ to all 24 ports, a 480W power supply, and two cooling fans to handle the additional thermal load. Choose the FPoE if you need to power access points, cameras, or phones directly from the switch.
