Fortinet 234F. Rugged Indoor/Outdoor Wi-Fi 6 Access Point

The FortiAP 234F is an enterprise-class Wi-Fi 6 (802.11ax) access point built to a rugged, weather-resistant spec, making it equally at home mounted indoors or on an exterior wall or pole. Its tri-radio design pairs 2x2 MU-MIMO Wi-Fi 6 with a dedicated 24/7 dual-band security scanning radio, and internal antennas keep the enclosure sealed for outdoor use. Dual GbE ports and 802.3at PoE simplify install in courtyards, warehouses, and mixed indoor/outdoor campuses.
