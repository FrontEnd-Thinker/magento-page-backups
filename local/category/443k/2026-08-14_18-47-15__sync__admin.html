Fortinet 443K. Wi-Fi 7 speed, security-fabric simplicity.

The FortiAP 443K brings Wi-Fi 7 (802.11be) performance to high-density indoor environments, pairing tri-band 4x4 MU-MIMO radios with a dedicated 24/7 RF scanning radio for always-on wireless security monitoring. Dual multigigabit Ethernet ports keep the network from becoming the bottleneck, while native integration with FortiGate or FortiEdge Cloud delivers unified wired/wireless policy from a single console. Built for busy enterprise floors, campuses, and branch offices that need maximum throughput without sacrificing built-in threat protection.

443K at a Glance

Wireless Performance

Wi-Fi 7 (802.11be) tri-band radios (2.4/5/6 GHz), each 4x4 MU-MIMO.
Up to 1.148 Gbps on 2.4GHz, 8.648 Gbps on 5GHz, and 11.53 Gbps on 6GHz (per-band PHY rates).
OFDMA, UL/DL MU-MIMO, BSS Coloring, and Target Wake Time for dense-client efficiency.

Antennas & Coverage

10 external RP-SMA antennas: 4x dual-band (2.4/5GHz), 4x 6GHz-only, and 2x tri-band scanning.
Indoor-rated for offices, classrooms, and high-density enterprise floors.
Integrated BLE/ZigBee and GPS antennas for locationing and asset tracking.

Connectivity & Power

2x Multigigabit RJ45 ports (100M up to 10GbE) plus USB 3.0 and RS-232 console.
Powered by 802.3bt PoE for full performance, or dual 802.3at PoE with current sharing.
Max power draw 41.7W.

Management & Security

Managed by an integrated FortiGate WLAN controller or the FortiEdge Cloud portal.
Built-in WIDS/WIPS, spectrum analyzer, and 24/7 dedicated RF scanning.
Up to 24 SSIDs and 512 clients per radio, UL2043 plenum-rated.
