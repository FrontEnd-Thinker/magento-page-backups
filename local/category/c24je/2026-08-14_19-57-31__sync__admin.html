Fortinet C24JE. Discreet wall-plate Wi-Fi, built for FortiGate-secured networks.

The FortiAP C24JE is a specialized wall-plate access point from Fortinet's FortiAP-C series, designed to mount directly over a standard electrical junction box for a clean, in-wall footprint. It supports dual-band 802.11ac Wave 2 wireless with PoE passthrough for a connected device such as an IP phone, and is managed through FortiGate or FortiCloud. C24JE at a Glance

Wireless & Form Factor

Dual-band 802.11ac Wave 2 wireless with internal antennas.
Wall-plate design mounts directly over a standard electrical junction box for a clean, discreet install.
Fits North America, EU, UK, China, Japan, AUS/NZ, and Korea box standards.

Connectivity & Power

WAN/PoE port plus LAN1, LAN2, and LAN3 (LAN3 doubles as a PoE passthrough port).
Powered by 802.3af (15.4W) or 802.3at (25.5W).
When powered via 802.3at, it can pass through up to 802.3af-level PoE to a downstream device like an IP phone.

Deployment

Ideal for hospitality rooms, classrooms, and offices that need in-wall Wi-Fi without a visible ceiling AP.
Console port included for local configuration and troubleshooting.
Operating range approximately 0-40°C ambient.

Management & Status

Managed via a FortiGate WLAN controller (CAPWAP) or FortiCloud provisioning.
