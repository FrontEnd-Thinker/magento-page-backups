Rack Mount Kits at a Glance

Compatibility

Cisco Meraki's official kit, MA-RCKMNT-KIT-1, is built for the mid-size MX85, MX95, and MX105. If you're running a smaller MX67, MX68, or MX75, reach out to our team first — those models are primarily marketed as desktop-only, and we'll confirm the right fit before you order.

What's Included

A pair of mounting ears that attach to the sides of the MX chassis, plus the screws needed to secure them to both the appliance and your rack rails.

Installation

Screw the ears onto the chassis, then mount into any standard 19" rack. Front-facing ports and status LEDs stay fully visible after install.

Rack Space Planning

Budget 1U per appliance. There is no official side-by-side dual-unit kit for the MX line.
