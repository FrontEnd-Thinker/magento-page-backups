XGS 2300 FAQ

The Base License covers the core firewall, networking, SD-WAN, wireless, and unlimited site-to-site and remote access VPN. Standard Protection adds Network Protection (IPS, advanced threat protection, Security Heartbeat, and SD-RED VPN) and Web Protection (web filtering and application control), plus Enhanced Support. Xstream Protection includes everything in Standard and adds Zero-Day Protection with cloud sandboxing and machine learning file analysis. Most buyers choose Xstream for full coverage, while Standard is a fit when you do not need sandboxing.

Sophos rates the XGS 2300 for roughly 300 to 500 users, which places it in the mid-range for growing businesses, multi-site organizations, and distributed edge deployments. Real headroom depends on how many security services you run at once and your internet speed.
