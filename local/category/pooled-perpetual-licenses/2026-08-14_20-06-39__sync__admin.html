Pooled, Perpetual, and Spike at a Glance

Pooled Licensing

Pooled licenses live in a central license pool managed by the SonicWall Central Management Server (CMS). CMS monitors real-time session demand across every SMA 1000 appliance in the cluster and allocates licenses where they are needed, automatically. A site that goes quiet at 5pm hands its licensed capacity to a time zone coming online. Organizations with multiple locations typically need fewer total licenses under a pooled model than under per-appliance licensing.

Perpetual Licensing

A perpetual license does not expire. Once purchased, the user capacity it enables stays with your SMA appliance indefinitely. An active support contract is required to receive firmware updates and technical support, but the license itself does not need to be renewed on an annual cycle. Perpetual licenses are well suited for environments where long-term cost certainty matters more than flexible scaling.

Spike Licensing

Spike licenses add short-term capacity in 5-day increments. They are designed for planned events: a major merger migration, a seasonal surge, an all-hands remote day, or any period where you know peak concurrent usage will temporarily exceed your permanent license count. Spike licenses are available in 1,000-user and 50,000-user packs and can be applied through CMS without touching individual appliance configurations.

License Management

All SMA license types are registered and tracked through MySonicWall. Pooled and spike license allocation is automated by CMS in real time. Real-time dashboards in CMS show active sessions against licensed capacity for every node in the cluster, so you can catch headroom problems before users start hitting connection limits.

Not sure whether pooled or per-appliance licensing is right for you?

Organizations with a single SMA appliance are typically better served by standard stackable user licenses. Pooled licensing pays off when you have two or more SMA 1000 appliances with meaningfully different peak-usage times. Our team can run the numbers for your specific situation.

SMA Pooled and Perpetual Licenses FAQ

Pooled licenses are subscription-based and sit in a central license pool managed by CMS. CMS distributes them dynamically across all SMA 1000 appliances in the cluster based on real-time session demand. Perpetual licenses are a one-time purchase that never expires. They are assigned to a specific appliance and remain in place as long as you own and operate the appliance.

A spike license is a short-term capacity add-on that activates for a defined 5-day window. It is used when you expect a temporary surge in concurrent remote users, such as during a planned migration, a company-wide remote work event, or peak seasonal periods. Spike licenses are available in 1,000-user and 50,000-user packs and are managed through CMS without requiring individual appliance reconfiguration.
