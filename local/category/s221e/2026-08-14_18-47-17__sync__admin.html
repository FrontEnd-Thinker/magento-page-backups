S221E at a Glance

Wireless Performance

802.11ac Wave 2 dual-radio design covering 2.4GHz and 5GHz concurrently.
Up to 300 Mbps on 2.4GHz and 867 Mbps on 5GHz (~1.17 Gbps aggregate).
MU-MIMO, transmit beamforming, LDPC, and frame aggregation for smoother multi-device performance.

Antennas & Coverage

4 internal PIFA antennas (3 dBi @2.4GHz, 5 dBi @5GHz) tuned for office and classroom ceiling coverage.
Indoor use only; no IP ingress rating.
Built for medium-density spaces rather than long-range or stadium-scale deployments.

Onboard Security

Embedded UTM engine runs IPS, antivirus, web filtering, and application control right on the AP.
Built-in WIDS/WIPS, rogue AP detection, and spectrum analyzer.
Extends FortiGate-grade protection to sites without a local firewall.

Power & Management

Dual redundant PoE ports (802.3af/802.3at); optional AC adapter or Fortinet power injector.
Managed via an integrated FortiGate WLAN controller or FortiCloud.
Supports Local-Bridge, Mesh, and Tunnel SSID modes, up to 16 SSIDs.
