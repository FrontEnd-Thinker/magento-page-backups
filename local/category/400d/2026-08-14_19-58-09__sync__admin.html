Fortinet 400D. Legacy Data Center Application Delivery Controller (D-Series, End-of-Support)

The FortiADC 400D is a previous-generation Fortinet application delivery controller from the D-series lineup, originally built for larger data center server load balancing, SSL offload, and application acceleration with hardware (ASIC) SSL assist. According to publicly available Fortinet lifecycle trackers, Fortinet ended new sales of the 400D in October 2019, and vendor support ended in October 2024 — meaning the platform is now past its supported life. The current FortiADC catalog centers on the F-series (60F through 5000F), which succeeds the D-series with higher throughput and modern SSL acceleration. The 400D may still turn up on the secondary/refurbished market for like-for-like replacement in existing 400D environments — new production deployments should use a current-generation F-series model instead. Confirm exact support status with Fortinet before purchasing.

Load Balancing & SSL
