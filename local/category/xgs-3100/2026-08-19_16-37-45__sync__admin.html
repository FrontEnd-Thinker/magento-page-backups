XGS 3100 at a Glance

Performance

47 Gbps firewall throughput and 10.5 Gbps IPS throughput, with 9 Gbps NGFW and roughly 2 Gbps of full threat protection. VPN throughput runs to about 2.47 Gbps, so multi-gig internet and heavy remote access have room to breathe.

Connectivity

Built in: 8x GE copper, 2x SFP fiber, and 2x SFP+ 10GbE fiber. A Flexi Port expansion bay lets you add copper, fiber, PoE, or bypass modules later instead of buying new hardware, and an optional second power supply adds redundancy.

Xstream Architecture

A dual-processor design pairs a multi-core CPU with a dedicated Xstream Flow Processor. It accelerates TLS 1.3 inspection and streaming DPI, keeping deep inspection fast even when nearly all of your web traffic is encrypted.

Management & Licensing

Every XGS 3100 is managed from Sophos Central, the same cloud console for reporting, policy, and SD-WAN. Choose a Standard or Xstream Protection bundle in 1, 3, or 5 year terms to fit your security needs and budget.
