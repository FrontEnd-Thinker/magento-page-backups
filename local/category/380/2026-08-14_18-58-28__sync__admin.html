SonicWall TZ380. Gen 8 firewall with multi-gig SFP+ for small organizations.

The SonicWall TZ380 is a Gen 8 desktop next-generation firewall designed for small offices and lean branches that are ready to move beyond gigabit uplinks. Its two 5G/2.5G/1G multi-gig SFP+ ports make it future-ready for multi-gig ISP connections and high-speed switches, while eight 1GbE copper ports cover wired LAN devices. Running SonicOS 8 with TLS 1.3, Deep Packet Inspection, Capture ATP, and Secure SD-WAN, it delivers 3.5 Gbps of firewall throughput and 1.5 Gbps of threat prevention throughput. The optional secondary power supply adds redundancy for locations that cannot afford unplanned downtime.

TZ380 at a Glance

Performance

3.5 Gbps firewall inspection, 1.5 Gbps threat prevention, 2 Gbps IPS throughput, 600 Mbps DPI-SSL. Supports 15,000 new connections per second and up to 1,100,000 concurrent SPI connections.

Interfaces

Eight 1GbE copper ports, two 5G/2.5G/1G multi-gig SFP+ slots, USB Type-A for cellular failover, USB Type-C for console access, and one micro-USB console port. Optional expandable storage up to 512 GB.

VPN and SD-WAN

Up to 200 site-to-site IPSec VPN tunnels, 5 concurrent IPSec VPN clients (200 max), 2 SSL VPN licenses (100 max), Secure SD-WAN, ZTNA via Cloud Secure Edge connector, and route-based VPN.

Security Services

Gateway Anti-Virus, Anti-Spyware, IPS, Application Control, Content Filtering Service 5.0, Capture ATP with RTDMI, DNS Security, Advanced DNS Filtering, and Comprehensive Anti-Spam. Active subscription required.

TZ380 FAQ

The TZ380 is a Gen 8 model running SonicOS 8, with higher throughput at 3.5 Gbps, two multi-gig 5G/2.5G/1G SFP+ ports, a larger maximum connection table (1.1 million versus 900,000), and an optional redundant power supply. It is the recommended upgrade path from the TZ370.

The TZ380 SFP+ ports support 5G, 2.5G, and 1G operation. SonicWall-branded SR multi-mode fiber, LR single-mode fiber, direct-attach copper twinax cables, and 1G copper RJ45 modules are all supported. Firewalls.com carries compatible modules.

Yes. The TZ380 supports an optional secondary power supply for added redundancy. The secondary power supply is sold separately and provides failover protection if the primary supply fails.

The TZ380W adds a built-in dual-band Wi-Fi 6 (802.11ax) radio. The firewall throughput, ports, and security features are identical. Choose the TZ380W if you need integrated Wi-Fi 6 without a separate access point.

The TZ380 delivers 1.5 Gbps of threat prevention throughput with Gateway Anti-Virus, Anti-Spyware, Intrusion Prevention, and Application Control all active simultaneously. This is a meaningful increase over the Gen 7 TZ370 at 1 Gbps.
