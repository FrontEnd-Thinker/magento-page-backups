Standard Protection covers the base firewall features plus Xstream SD-WAN, ZTNA, and network visibility. Xstream Protection adds the full threat protection stack on top: IPS, advanced threat protection, deep learning, real-time sandboxing, TLS 1.3 inspection, and DNS protection, along with Sophos Central management. For most networks that handle encrypted traffic and need active threat inspection, Xstream Protection is the recommended bundle.

Sophos Central is a single cloud console that manages all your Sophos products, including XGS firewalls, switches, and wireless access points. You can configure changes and push them to a group of firewalls at once or manage each appliance individually. Central also handles scheduled firmware updates, cloud backup with up to five stored copies, and zero-touch deployment for new hardware shipped directly to a remote office. Central management is included at no extra cost with any Xstream Protection or Standard Protection bundle.
