Switch 14-48-FPoE at a Glance

Massive PoE+ Capacity

802.3af/at PoE+ on all 48 copper ports with up to 30W per port and 740W of total PoE budget. Supports per-port PoE scheduling (Time Management Options), power class configuration, and priority-based power feeding to protect the most critical devices if the budget is reached.

Performance

176 Gbps non-blocking switching capacity with a 32K MAC table and 2M packet buffer. Four 10G SFP+ uplinks aggregate traffic from 48 active PoE+ ports without becoming a bottleneck, even in high-density wireless deployments with multiple APs running simultaneously.

Management

Full integration with SonicWall firewalls and Wireless Network Manager for centralized VLAN configuration, per-port PoE management, switch auto-discovery, and zero-touch deployment via Capture Security Center. Standalone web GUI, CLI, SSH, SNMP, and syslog are also available.

Power and Form Factor

900W fixed power supply (100-240 VAC). Maximum power consumption: 885W. Three cooling fans manage thermal load at full PoE capacity. 1U rackmount design: 440 x 310 x 44mm, 4.8 kg. Minimum 12-amp power circuit required. Includes a serial console port (RJ45).
