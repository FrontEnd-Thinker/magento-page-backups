Fusion at a Glance

Adaptive Defense EDR

Zero-Trust Application Service classifies every running process, while behavioral monitoring and Panda's threat hunting team detect and investigate advanced threats. Layers on top of your existing AV without conflict or redundancy.

Full RMM Platform

Complete Panda Systems Management capabilities: remote monitoring, remote desktop access, scripted task deployment, patch management for OS and third-party applications, and hardware and software inventory across all managed devices.

Works Alongside Existing AV

Fusion's EDR component is designed to complement an existing endpoint protection product. Organizations committed to a specific AV vendor can add advanced detection and response without disrupting their current security stack.

Single Agent, Unified Console

One agent handles EDR monitoring and IT management tasks. IT and security teams operate from the same Aether console, share device inventory data, and apply coordinated responses when a security incident requires both IT action and threat containment.

Fusion FAQ

Panda Fusion includes Adaptive Defense (EDR only) plus Systems Management. Panda Fusion 360 replaces the EDR-only component with Adaptive Defense 360, which bundles full EPP and EDR in one. Fusion is the right choice for organizations that want to keep their existing antivirus and add EDR and RMM capability on top of it. Fusion 360 is for those who want to consolidate everything, including antivirus, into a single agent and subscription.

Adaptive Defense is designed to coexist with third-party antivirus products. It focuses on behavioral monitoring and process classification rather than signature-based scanning, which avoids the typical conflicts that arise when two AV products compete for access to the same file system resources. That said, compatibility can vary by specific product combination. A Firewalls.com specialist can help you verify compatibility with your existing AV before you purchase.
