433G at a Glance

Wireless Performance

Wi-Fi 6E (802.11ax) tri-radio design with dedicated 2.4 GHz, 5 GHz, and 6 GHz radios.
4x4 MU-MIMO with OFDMA and BSS Coloring for dense client environments.
Data rates up to 1.148 Gbps (2.4 GHz), 2.402 Gbps (5 GHz), and 4.804 Gbps (6 GHz).

Connectivity & Power

Dual 5 Gigabit Ethernet (RJ45) ports with MDI/MDIX support.
Powered by 802.3bt PoE, or dual 802.3at PoE for current sharing.
External omni-directional antennas (6 GHz antennas integral to the unit).

Indoor Deployment

Purpose-built indoor access point rated for standard commercial environments (0°C to 50°C).
Ceiling, T-rail, and wall mounting options with included mount kit.
UL2043 plenum-rated for above-ceiling installations.

Management & Security

Centrally managed through any FortiGate integrated wireless controller or the FortiLAN Cloud portal.
Built-in Firewall, IPS, Application Control, and Web Filtering extend the Security Fabric to the wireless edge.
Integrated BLE/Zigbee radio for beacon and locationing applications.
