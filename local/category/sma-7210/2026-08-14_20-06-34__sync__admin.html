SMA 7210 at a Glance

Performance

5.0 Gbps SSL VPN throughput with AES-NI hardware acceleration and SSL offload. 16GB DDR4 handles 10,000 concurrent sessions without degradation. Dual 1TB SATA drives in RAID 1 protect logs and configuration. MTBF rated at 129,601 hours.

Hardware

Six 1GbE copper ports plus two 10Gb SFP+ uplinks for high-bandwidth data center connections. Dual hot-swappable power supplies at 114W let you replace a failed PSU without taking the appliance offline. 1U form factor identical to the SMA 6210.

Access Security

FIPS 140-2 Level 2 certified with optional FIPS add-on for anti-tamper protection. Advanced Endpoint Control (EPC) checks every device before granting access. Zero-Trust policy engine limits each user and device to exactly the resources they are authorized to reach.

Scale and Management

Pairs with Central Management Server (CMS) to manage up to 100 SMA 1000 appliances in a global mesh. CMS pools licenses dynamically, redirecting capacity from idle regions to peak-demand sites. Active/Active HA enables zero-downtime firmware upgrades at any session count.

Comparing the SMA 7210 with the 6210 or 8200v?

The 7210 is the highest-capacity physical appliance in the SMA 1000 Series. If you are under 2,000 concurrent users, the 6210 may be a better fit on cost. If you need cloud or hypervisor deployment, review the 8200v. Our comparison page lays out all three side by side.

SMA 7210 FAQ

The SonicWall SMA 7210 is the top-tier physical appliance in the SMA 1000 Series. It is a 1U rack-mount secure access gateway that supports up to 10,000 concurrent user sessions at 5 Gbps SSL VPN throughput. It targets large enterprises and MSSPs that need carrier-grade remote access capacity in a single appliance.

The SMA 7210 supports five times more concurrent users than the SMA 6210 (10,000 versus 2,000) and delivers nearly four times the throughput (5.0 Gbps versus 1.3 Gbps). It also adds two 10Gb SFP+ uplinks and dual hot-swappable power supplies not present on the 6210. For organizations with a projected peak under 2,000 sessions the 6210 is typically better value. At 2,000 users and above, a single 7210 is usually less expensive than two 6210 appliances in an HA pair.

Yes. The SMA 7210 supports Active/Passive and Active/Active high availability. In Active/Active mode sessions are distributed across both units and firmware can be upgraded on one node at a time without dropping connections. With Central Management Server, you can also build multi-site active-active clusters that share session state globally.

Yes. The SonicWall Central Management Server (CMS) manages physical SMA 6210 and 7210 appliances alongside SMA 8200v virtual instances in a single mesh of up to 100 nodes. CMS pools user licenses dynamically across the entire cluster, so capacity follows real demand rather than being locked to a single site.

Like all SMA 1000 appliances, the SMA 7210 ships without user licenses. You purchase stackable SMA User Licenses separately in 5-user, 100-user, or 5,000-user packs. The 5,000-user pack is available for the SMA 7210 and 8200v only. Capture ATP for SMA is an optional subscription add-on for cloud-based file scanning.
