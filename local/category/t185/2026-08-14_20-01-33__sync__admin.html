T185 at a Glance

Performance

UTM throughput (full scan with AV, IPS, App Control): 1.83 Gbps. Firewall throughput: 7.90 Gbps (UDP 1518). VPN throughput: 2.20 Gbps (UDP 1518). IPS throughput: 2.40 Gbps. HTTPS inspection: 1.00 Gbps. Antivirus: 2.80 Gbps. Firewall throughput (IMIX): 5.70 Gbps. VPN throughput (IMIX): 1.14 Gbps. Performance validated using NetSecOPEN standardized testing methodology.

Connectivity

4x 1Gb Ethernet and 4x 2.5Gb Ethernet (2 of the 2.5Gb ports are 802.3at PoE+ capable to power WatchGuard APs or other devices). 1x SFP/SFP+ (accepts 1Gb SFP or 10Gb SFP+ transceivers). 4GB of RAM. 50 EDR Core sensors included. Built-in, highly reliable integrated power supply. Kensington Lock slot for physical security. 1 Serial port and 2 USB-A 3.0 ports.

VPN and SD-WAN

100 Branch Office VPN (site-to-site) tunnels. 100 Mobile VPN connections. Site-to-site VPN over IKEv2, IPSec, policy and route-based tunnels, and TLS hub and spoke. Remote access over IKEv2, IPSec, L2TP, and TLS. SD-WAN with multi-WAN failover, dynamic path selection, and jitter, loss, and latency measurement. Active/passive and active/active high availability clustering. Unlimited VLANs.

Security Services

Stateful packet inspection, TLS decryption, and proxy firewall. Basic Security Suite includes IPS, Gateway AV, Application Control, WebBlocker, spamBlocker, and Reputation Enabled Defense. Total Security Suite adds APT Blocker, IntelligentAV, DNSWatch, ThreatSync (XDR), and 50 EDR Core sensors. Over 100 dashboards and reports in WatchGuard Cloud, including PCI and HIPAA compliance. 4 WSM management licenses included.

T185 FAQ

The T185 targets mid-market businesses, regional branch offices, and retail environments with up to 150 users and heavy traffic loads. It is WatchGuard's fastest tabletop Firebox, with performance validated using the NetSecOPEN standardized testing methodology, and built for sites where the T145 would be undersized. If your primary location or largest branch is pushing more than 75 users and fast ISP circuits, the T185 is where to look before moving to rack hardware.

The T185 delivers 1.83 Gbps UTM throughput versus 710 Mbps on the T145, 7.90 Gbps firewall throughput versus 3.90 Gbps, and 100 Branch Office VPN tunnels versus 25. It also provides four 2.5Gb ports (versus one on the T145) and two of those ports are PoE+ capable. The T185 includes 50 EDR Core sensors versus 20, and 4 WSM management licenses. The T185 does not have a wireless radio, where the T145-W does.

Two of the 2.5Gb ports on the T185 are 802.3at PoE+ compliant at 2.5Gbps link speeds, a feature unique to the T185 in the tabletop lineup. Each port provides enough power to run a WatchGuard AP or other small PoE-powered device. This removes the need for a separate PoE injector at sites where you want a WatchGuard AP co-located with the firewall and connected at full 2.5Gb speed.
