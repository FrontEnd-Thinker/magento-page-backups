MR44 at a Glance

Radios & Antennas

Four radios: asymmetric 2.4GHz 2x2:2 and 5GHz 4x4:4 client radios, a dedicated dual-band WIDS/WIPS and RF scanning radio, and an integrated BLE radio. Internal omnidirectional antennas.

Performance & Capacity

2.7 Gbps aggregate throughput (2,402 Mbps @5GHz, 287 Mbps @2.4GHz), with MU-MIMO and OFDMA improving efficiency for mid-density client loads.

Design & Deployment

Indoor desktop, ceiling, or wall mount with a 2.5GbE multigig port; runs on 802.3at PoE (30W) or an 802.3af low-power fallback mode (15W).

Management & Security

Cloud-managed through the Meraki dashboard with built-in Air Marshal WIDS/WIPS and BLE-based location analytics.

MR44 FAQ

The MR44 supports 802.11ax (Wi-Fi 6), with a 2.4ghz 2x2:2 + 5ghz 4x4:4 radio configuration delivering up to 2.7 gbps aggregate.

Capacity depends on RF environment and channel width, but the MR44’s radio design is built to match its target deployment — from light per-room use to high-density venues. Our engineers can help size your specific site.

The MR44 is designed for indoor mount, 2.5gbe port installation. See the datasheet or installation guide for exact bracket and clearance requirements.

Most MR44 deployments run best on 802.3at (PoE+); some radios or streams may be reduced on standard 802.3af power. Check the datasheet for the exact power budget.

Like every Meraki access point, the MR44 is provisioned, monitored, and updated entirely from the cloud-based Meraki dashboard — no on-site controller required.
