Sophos sizes its virtual firewalls by the number of vCPU cores the license allows, not by users. This tier lets you assign 2 cores to the virtual machine. Two cores is a comfortable fit for branch offices, lab and test networks, and smaller cloud workloads. If you expect heavy TLS inspection or high throughput, step up to a 4-core or larger license.
