Fortinet VM04. Virtual Email Security Gateway — 4 vCPU

FortiMail VM04 is licensed for up to 4 vCPUs and is built for mid to large enterprises that need substantially more throughput and mailbox capacity than the VM01/VM02 tiers provide. It supports up to 500 protected email domains and roughly 1,500 server-mode mailboxes, with email routing throughput of roughly 306,000 messages per hour — nearly 5x that of VM02. VM04 also raises the maximum supported RAM to 16 GB and the minimum vNIC allowance to match higher-tier models, giving it enough headroom for full Data Loss Prevention, Centralized Quarantine, and Enterprise ATP scanning at scale.
