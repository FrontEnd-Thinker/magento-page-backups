What GMS Did and What Replaces It

GMS to NSM: What You Need to Know

What GMS Provided

SonicWall GMS gave distributed enterprises and service providers a single console to centrally manage SonicWall firewall, wireless, email security, and secure remote access appliances. Core capabilities included policy configuration across thousands of nodes, VPN deployment and consolidation, real-time monitoring with alerting, scheduled configuration and firmware management, and compliance reporting. It was available as a software install, hardware appliance, or virtual appliance.

Why SonicWall Ended GMS

GMS was designed for a generation of on-premises management infrastructure. As firewall estates moved to include Gen 7 and Gen 8 appliances with SonicOS and SonicOSX, SonicWall built NSM from the ground up to support both firmware generations, cloud-delivered management, AI-driven insights, and multi-tenancy for MSPs. GMS licenses reached Last Day Order on October 1, 2025, and SonicWall is directing all customers to NSM for ongoing management.

NSM: The Direct Replacement

NSM delivers centralized policy management, zero-touch deployment, real-time monitoring, and compliance reporting for the full SonicWall firewall estate. It supports Gen 6 firewalls running SonicOS 6.5.x or higher and all Gen 7 devices running SonicOSX 7.0 or higher. Cloud (SaaS) and on-premises deployment options are available. Basic firewall management is included with firewall support subscriptions at no additional charge, with paid NSM tiers adding extended reporting and log analytics.

How to Start the Transition

Our team can review your current GMS deployment, map your node count and feature requirements to the right NSM license tier, and walk you through the migration steps. NSM on-premises runs on VMware ESXi, Hyper-V, KVM, and Azure. NSM SaaS requires no infrastructure. Both options are available through Firewalls.com with the same PO and Net 30 billing your organization uses today.
