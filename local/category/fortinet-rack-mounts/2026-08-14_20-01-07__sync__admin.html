Rack Mount Kits at a Glance

Compatibility

One tray, multiple footprints: Fortinet's SP-RACKTRAY-02 uses molded cutouts to accommodate several FortiGate desktop chassis sizes across the E and F Series, including the 40F, 60F, and 80F.

What's Included

The tray/shelf itself, rack ears, and the mounting screws needed to secure the tray to your rack rails.

Installation

Your FortiGate simply drops into the molded tray — no screws needed to seat the chassis. A screwdriver is only needed to attach the tray to your rack.

Rack Space Planning

Each tray uses 1U. There's no verified official two-unit-per-1U Fortinet tray, so plan one rack slot per appliance and keep the chassis vents clear.

Not sure which FortiGate you have?

The universal tray covers most E and F Series desktop models, but it's worth confirming your exact FortiGate before ordering.
