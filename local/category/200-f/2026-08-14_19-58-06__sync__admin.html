200F at a Glance

Performance

L4/L7 throughput of up to 3 Gbps / 2.2 Gbps, with 100,000 Layer 4 connections per second and 300,000 HTTP requests per second.
Supports up to 5 million concurrent Layer 4 connections, suited for multiple production application pools.

Load Balancing & SSL

Layer 4/7 load balancing with round robin, weighted round robin, least connections, and shortest-response methods, plus included Global Server Load Balancing and Link Load Balancing.
Software-based SSL offload delivers up to 1,000 SSL TPS (2048-bit keys) and 1 Gbps of bulk encryption throughput.

Connectivity

4x GE RJ45 plus 2x GE SFP network interfaces in a 1U rack-mount appliance, with 64 GB SSD onboard storage and support for up to 10 Virtual Domains.
Management via HTTPS, SSH CLI, direct console, and SNMP.

High Availability & Form Factor

Supports active-active and active-passive HA failover for continuous application availability.
Compact 1U rack-mount chassis with a single power supply, suited for small-to-mid data center and campus deployments.
