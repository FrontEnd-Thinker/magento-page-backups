Central Device Encryption at a Glance

Managed from Sophos Central

Set an encryption policy once in the Sophos Central cloud console and it applies as users log in. There are no key management servers to stand up and no back end to babysit. If you already run Sophos endpoints, encryption lives right next to the rest of your security policies.

Native BitLocker and FileVault

Sophos does not invent its own crypto. It drives the encryption already built into your operating systems, BitLocker on Windows and FileVault on Mac, so you get vendor supported full disk encryption on boot and fixed data volumes.

Recovery and compliance

Recovery keys are escrowed securely in Sophos Central, and locked out users can pull their own key from the Self Service Portal. Encryption status reporting gives you the proof auditors ask for when a laptop goes missing.

Deployment and licensing

Policies push out remotely in a couple of clicks. On Windows the encryption agent ships with the standard Sophos Central endpoint installer, so there is nothing extra to deploy. Licensing is per user, in seat tiers, on one, two, or three year terms.

Not sure encryption is the layer you need first?

Device Encryption protects data at rest on lost or stolen machines. If you are also weighing endpoint protection, XDR, or email security, line them up side by side before you buy.
