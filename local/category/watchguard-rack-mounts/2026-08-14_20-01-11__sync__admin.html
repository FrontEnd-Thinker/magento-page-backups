Rack Mount Kits at a Glance

Compatibility

Kits are matched by model group: RM-WG-T3 (T10/T15), RM-WG-T6 (T20/T40), RM-WG-T2 (T30/T50), RM-WG-T5 (T35/T55), RM-WG-T4 (T70), and RM-WG-T7 (T80/T85).

What's Included

A bracket/shelf sized to your Firebox chassis and the screws needed to mount it, with front port access preserved.

Installation

A standard screwdriver install — attach the bracket to the appliance, then mount into your rack rails.

Rack Space Planning

Each kit uses 1U. There's no confirmed dual-unit kit for the T-Series, so plan one rack slot per Firebox.
