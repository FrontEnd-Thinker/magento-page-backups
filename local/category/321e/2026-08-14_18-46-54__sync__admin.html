Fortinet 321E. Indoor 802.11ac Wave 2 Access Point

The FortiAP 321E is a dual-radio, medium-density 3x3 802.11ac Wave 2 access point built for offices, classrooms, and retail environments. With MU-MIMO support and throughput up to 1,300 Mbps on the 5GHz band, it delivers reliable enterprise Wi-Fi for everyday indoor deployments. Note: the 321E is a discontinued model, so confirm current support status with a Firewalls.com rep before standardizing on it for new rollouts.

321E FAQ

The FortiAP 321E is an indoor access point designed for T-bar ceiling or wall mounting in offices, classrooms, and retail spaces. It is not rated for outdoor or harsh-environment installation.

The 321E is a dual-radio 802.11ac Wave 2 access point with 3x3 MU-MIMO, delivering data rates up to 1,300 Mbps on the 5GHz band.

The 321E is powered over a single GE RJ45 port using 802.3af or 802.3at PoE, which can be supplied by a PoE switch, injector, or a FortiGate's built-in PoE ports.

Yes. The 321E is managed centrally by the integrated WLAN controller in any FortiGate firewall, or through Fortinet's cloud-based provisioning and management portal.

The 321E is a discontinued model that is approaching or past its published end-of-support date. It remains a solid choice for matching an existing 321E deployment; for new installs, ask about current-generation FortiAP options like the 421E or 431F.
