MR46E at a Glance

Radios & Antennas

Identical RF chain to the MR46 — 4x4:4 MIMO/OFDMA on both bands plus dedicated scanning and BLE radios — but with six external antenna connectors (one per radio chain) supporting Meraki's MA-ANT-3 dipole, patch, panel, and downtilt antenna lineup.

Performance & Capacity

2.98 Gbps aggregate throughput, matching the MR46, with coverage shaped by the external antennas selected for the deployment.

Design & Deployment

Indoor mount rated for a wider operating temperature range (up to 122°F/50°C) for harsher indoor environments like warehouses. Includes a 2.5GbE port and requires 802.3at PoE (30W); antennas sold separately.

Management & Security

Cloud-managed through the Meraki dashboard with built-in Air Marshal WIDS/WIPS and BLE scanning.

MR46E FAQ

The MR46E supports 802.11ax (Wi-Fi 6), with a 4x4:4 mu-mimo, 6 external antenna ports radio configuration delivering up to 2.98 gbps aggregate.

Capacity depends on RF environment and channel width, but the MR46E’s radio design is built to match its target deployment — from light per-room use to high-density venues. Our engineers can help size your specific site.

The MR46E is designed for indoor mount, wide operating temp range installation. See the datasheet or installation guide for exact bracket and clearance requirements.

Most MR46E deployments run best on 802.3at (PoE+); some radios or streams may be reduced on standard 802.3af power. Check the datasheet for the exact power budget.

Like every Meraki access point, the MR46E is provisioned, monitored, and updated entirely from the cloud-based Meraki dashboard — no on-site controller required.
