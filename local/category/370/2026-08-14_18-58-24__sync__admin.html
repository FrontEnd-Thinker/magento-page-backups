TZ370 at a Glance

Performance

3 Gbps firewall inspection, 1 Gbps threat prevention, 1.5 Gbps IPS throughput, 500 Mbps DPI-SSL. Supports 9,000 new connections per second and up to 900,000 concurrent SPI connections.

Interfaces

Eight 1GbE copper ports, two USB 3.0 SuperSpeed ports for cellular failover, and one RJ45 console port. Optional internal M.2 storage up to 256 GB for logging, reporting, caching, and firmware backup.

VPN and SD-WAN

Up to 100 site-to-site IPSec VPN tunnels, 5 concurrent IPSec VPN clients (200 max), 2 SSL VPN licenses (100 max), Secure SD-WAN, Auto-Provision VPN, and route-based VPN with OSPF, RIP, and BGP.

Security Services

Gateway Anti-Virus, Anti-Spyware, IPS, Application Control, Content Filtering Service, Capture ATP with RTDMI, DPI-SSL, Comprehensive Anti-Spam, and Application Visualization. Requires active subscription.
