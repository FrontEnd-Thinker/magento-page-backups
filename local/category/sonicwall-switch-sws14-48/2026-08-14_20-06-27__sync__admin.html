Switch 14-48 at a Glance

Performance

176 Gbps non-blocking switching capacity with a 32K MAC address table and 2M packet buffer. Four 10G SFP+ uplinks move aggregated traffic to your core at 10 times the speed of a 1G uplink, preventing uplink congestion even at full port utilization.

Connectivity

48 x 1G RJ45 copper ports for wired workstations, servers, printers, and other devices in a single 1U chassis. Supports VLAN tagging for up to 4,096 VLANs, 802.3ad link aggregation, jumbo frames, and IGMP snooping for multicast traffic.

Management

Integrates with SonicWall firewalls and Wireless Network Manager for unified management, zero-touch deployment, and centralized VLAN configuration. Standalone management via web GUI, CLI, SSH, SNMP, and syslog is also supported. Includes a serial console port (RJ45).

Power and Form Factor

60W fixed power supply (100-240 VAC). Maximum power consumption: 44W. One cooling fan. 1U rackmount design: 440 x 260 x 44mm, 3.2 kg. MTBF rated at over 222,000 hours. No PoE; for PoE+ on all 48 ports, see the Switch 14-48-FPoE.

Switch 14-48 FAQ

The Switch 14-48 is best suited for medium to large office floors, wiring closets, and data center access layers where a high port count and fast uplinks are needed but PoE is not required. Common use cases include connecting wired workstations, servers, printers, VoIP adapters with their own power supplies, and other devices that plug into their own power outlets. It works well as a distribution or aggregation switch in a tiered network design.

The Switch 14-48 supports up to 4,096 VLANs using 802.1Q tagging, allowing you to segment traffic by department, device type, or compliance zone. Voice VLANs prioritize IP phone traffic, guest VLANs isolate visitor access, and MAC or IP-based ACLs further restrict port-level access. When paired with a SonicWall firewall, inter-VLAN routing and security policies are enforced at the firewall, giving you granular control over east-west traffic.

The Switch 14-48 does not support physical stacking with a dedicated stacking cable. However, multiple switch units can be daisy-chained via a SonicWall firewall in managed mode, where the firewall acts as the control point for all connected switches. For very large deployments, the 10G SFP+ uplinks can be used to connect multiple switches to a core switch or distribution layer for a hierarchical network design.

The Switch 14-48 includes 802.1X port-based authentication, MAC Authentication Bypass (MAB) for non-802.1X-capable devices, IP and MAC access control lists, DHCP snooping, port security, port isolation, and Denial of Service (DoS) attack prevention. When managed by a SonicWall firewall, the firewall's IPS, application control, gateway anti-virus, and botnet filtering policies apply to all traffic passing through it, extending protection to every connected device.

The Switch 14-48 has no PoE capability and uses a 60W power supply drawing only 44W maximum, making it a lean, cost-effective option for environments where devices have their own power. The Switch 14-48-FPoE adds 802.3at PoE+ across all 48 ports with 740W of total PoE budget, a 900W power supply, and three cooling fans to manage the higher thermal load. Choose the FPoE if you need to power access points, cameras, or phones from the switch.
