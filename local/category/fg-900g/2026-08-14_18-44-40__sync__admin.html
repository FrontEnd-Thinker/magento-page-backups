FortiGate 900G / 901G at a Glance

Performance

164 Gbps firewall throughput. 42 Gbps IPS throughput. 31 Gbps NGFW throughput. 30 Gbps full threat protection. 55 Gbps IPsec VPN throughput. 16.7 Gbps SSL inspection. 16 million concurrent sessions and 720,000 new sessions per second.

Interfaces

4x 25G SFP28 ultra-low latency slots. 4x 10GE SFP+ slots. 16x GE RJ45 switch ports. 8x GE SFP slots. Dedicated 2.5G HA port and 1G out-of-band management port. All data interfaces hardware-accelerated by the NP7 network processor. Two SFP SX transceivers included.

Hardware Acceleration

NP7 network processor and CP9 content processor deliver line-rate firewall and IPS without burdening the main CPU. The four 25G SFP28 ultra-low latency (ULL) slots bypass the integrated switch fabric for latency-sensitive workloads such as high-frequency trading and real-time analytics. Trusted Platform Module (TPM) included for hardware-rooted security.

Platform and Storage

FortiOS unifies firewall policy, SD-WAN, ZTNA enforcement, and FortiGuard services in a single operating system. The 900G ships without onboard storage. The 901G adds two 480 GB SSDs for local logging, reporting, and FortiAnalyzer analytics on-box. Both models ship in a 1U chassis with dual redundant hot-swappable AC power supplies. Operating temperature 32 to 104 F.
