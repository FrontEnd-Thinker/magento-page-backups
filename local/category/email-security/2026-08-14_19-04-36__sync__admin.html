Email Security at a Glance

Anti-Phishing and BEC Protection

Business email compromise (BEC) and spear-phishing attacks impersonate executives, vendors, and trusted contacts to trick employees into transferring money or credentials. AI-based inspection engines analyze sender reputation, header anomalies, display name spoofing, and link destinations in real time to catch these attacks even when they carry no malware payload and pass SPF, DKIM, and DMARC checks.

Advanced Threat Protection

Cloud sandbox technology detonates suspicious attachments in an isolated environment before delivery, catching zero-day malware and document-based exploits that signature scanners miss. Every major email security platform we carry includes sandboxing at the advanced tier. URL rewriting rewrites links at click-time so destinations are rechecked against live threat feeds even after the message has been delivered.

Spam Filtering and Bulk Mail Control

Effective spam filtering eliminates 99 percent or more of inbound junk mail using reputation scoring, content analysis, and machine learning models trained on billions of messages daily. End users can manage their own quarantine through a self-service portal, reducing help desk tickets while keeping legitimate mail flowing without administrator intervention.

Email Continuity and Archiving

Email continuity keeps your inbox accessible during an outage of your mail server or Microsoft 365, using a cloud-based emergency inbox so users can keep sending and receiving. Email archiving captures a tamper-proof copy of every inbound and outbound message for legal hold, e-discovery, and compliance requirements including HIPAA, FINRA, and SEC regulations.

Email Security FAQ

An email security gateway sits in front of your mail server or cloud inbox and filters all inbound and outbound messages before they reach users. It blocks spam, malware, phishing links, and malicious attachments using a combination of reputation data, content analysis, sandboxing, and machine learning. Cloud-based gateways require only an MX record change to deploy and add no hardware to manage. On-prem gateways give you full control over data residency and filtering rules.

Microsoft 365 Defender provides a baseline level of filtering, but independent testing consistently shows it misses a significant percentage of phishing emails and BEC attempts that a dedicated third-party gateway catches. Microsoft's filters are also a known target for attackers who craft campaigns specifically designed to evade them. A dedicated gateway like Barracuda, SonicWall, or Sophos Email adds a second layer of filtering with sandboxing and AI-based BEC detection that meaningfully reduces your phishing exposure.

Business email compromise is a form of fraud where an attacker impersonates a trusted person, such as a CEO, CFO, vendor, or attorney, to trick an employee into wiring money, changing payroll deposits, or sharing credentials. BEC attacks often carry no malicious attachments or links, which makes them invisible to traditional antivirus scanners. They rely entirely on social engineering and pass standard authentication checks. AI-based behavioral analysis is the most effective defense against BEC.

Email archiving captures and stores a tamper-proof copy of every inbound and outbound email, independent of the mail server, for a defined retention period. It is required by regulations including HIPAA, FINRA, SEC Rule 17a-4, and various state data retention laws. It is also essential for legal hold and e-discovery situations where you need to produce email records quickly and prove they have not been altered. Microsoft 365 retention policies are not a substitute because they can be modified or deleted by administrators.

A cloud-based email security gateway can be active in as little as a few hours. The process involves changing your domain's MX records to route mail through the gateway, configuring your mail server or Microsoft 365 tenant to accept mail only from the gateway's IP range, and tuning the filtering policies. Our configuration service handles the full deployment and hands off with all policies set and a verification that legitimate mail is flowing correctly.
