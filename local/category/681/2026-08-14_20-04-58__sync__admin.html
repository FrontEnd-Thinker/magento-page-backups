SonicWave 681 at a Glance

Flagship Wireless Performance

The SonicWave 681 leads the SonicWave 600 series with 8x8 MU-MIMO on the 5 GHz radio and 4x4 MU-MIMO on the 2.4 GHz radio. 802.11ax technology with 1024-QAM, uplink and downlink MU-MIMO, and OFDMA delivers up to 400% overall throughput improvement and 75% lower latency versus 802.11ac Wave 2. A 5 GbE uplink ensures the backhaul connection keeps pace with the radios in demanding, high-client environments.

Enterprise Security

A dedicated 802.11ax 1x1 scanning radio continuously monitors for rogue access points, performs passive RF scanning, and captures wireless packets for forensic analysis without interrupting client traffic. When connected to a SonicWall firewall on SonicOS 7.1.1 or higher, all wireless traffic passes through deep packet inspection, content filtering, application control, geo-IP filtering, botnet blocking, and Capture Advanced Threat Protection with RTDMI technology.

Hardware and Power

Twelve internal antennas serve the 2.4 GHz (4), 5 GHz (8), and scanning (1) radios, plus an integrated BLE/Bluetooth 5.0 antenna for IoT connectivity. The unit draws up to 34W and requires 802.3bt PoE++ from a compatible switch or injector. The SonicWave 681 is plenum rated for in-ceiling deployment and supports ceiling, wall, shelf, or desktop mounting configurations.

Cloud Management and Scale

SonicWall Wireless Network Manager (WNM) provides a single-pane-of-glass cloud platform for configuration, analytics, RF spectrum analysis, and Zero-Touch Deployment across all deployed SonicWave APs. The SonicExpress mobile app on iOS and Android automates discovery and onboarding. SonicWall WiFi Planner helps design optimal AP coverage before installation, reducing total cost of ownership for large-scale rollouts.

SonicWave 681 FAQ

The SonicWave 681 is built for high-density indoor spaces where a large number of wireless devices connect simultaneously. It is well suited for large corporate offices, university lecture halls, conference centers, hotel lobbies, auditoriums, and similar environments where throughput demands and concurrent client counts are highest. The 8x8 MU-MIMO 5 GHz radio and 5 GbE uplink give it significantly more headroom than the 641 or 621 in demanding deployments.

The SonicWave 681 requires 802.3bt PoE++ (also called PoE++ or 4PPoE), which delivers up to 60W over the Ethernet cable. The AP has a maximum power draw of 34W. This is a higher power requirement than the 621 and 641, which use the more common 802.3at PoE+ standard, so you will need a switch or injector that supports 802.3bt PoE++ to power the 681. Multi-Gigabit 802.3bt PoE++ injectors are available with select SKUs.

The SonicWave 681 can be managed through SonicWall Wireless Network Manager (WNM), a cloud platform that handles provisioning, configuration, analytics, RF spectrum monitoring, and Zero-Touch Deployment for all SonicWave APs in your environment. It can also be managed directly through a SonicWall firewall running SonicOS 7.1.1 or higher, which adds firewall-enforced security policies to all wireless traffic. The SonicExpress mobile app streamlines initial setup and onboarding.
