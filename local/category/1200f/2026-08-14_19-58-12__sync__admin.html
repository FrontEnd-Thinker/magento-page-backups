Fortinet FortiADC 1200F. High-Performance Application Delivery Controller

The FortiADC 1200F is a 1U application delivery controller built for data center load balancing, SSL/TLS offload, and application acceleration at scale. It pairs ASIC-based hardware SSL acceleration with advanced Layer 4/7 load balancing, Global Server Load Balancing (GSLB), and an integrated Web Application Firewall, giving it the highest connection-handling capacity in the 1U FortiADC lineup. With dual redundant power, TPM-backed security, and high-density 10GE connectivity, the 1200F is built for organizations that need serious load balancing and SSL offload headroom without stepping up to a 2U chassis.

FortiADC 1200F FAQ

The FortiADC 1200F delivers up to 40 Gbps of Layer 4 throughput and 30 Gbps of Layer 7 throughput, with support for up to 36 million concurrent connections and 1 million new connections per second.

The 1200F uses ASIC-based hardware SSL acceleration to deliver up to 35,000 SSL transactions per second (2K RSA keys) and 19 Gbps of SSL bulk encryption throughput, offloading CPU-intensive TLS processing from your backend servers.

Yes. The 1200F supports VRRP-based Active-Active and Active-Passive HA failover, along with included Global Server Load Balancing (GSLB) for multi-data-center resiliency and disaster recovery.

The 1200F ships with 8x 10GE SFP+ ports, 8x GE SFP ports, and 8x GE RJ45 ports, plus 2x GE RJ45 management ports, giving you high-density 10GbE connectivity alongside 1GbE ports for server-facing and client-facing links.

The 1200F is built for large data centers and hybrid IT environments that need the highest connection-handling and SSL offload capacity available in a 1U form factor, before stepping up to a 2U high-density platform.
