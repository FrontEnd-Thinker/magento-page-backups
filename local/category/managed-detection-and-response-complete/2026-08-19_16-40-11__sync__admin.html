MDR Complete at a Glance

What the team does

A Sophos security operations team monitors your environment 24/7, investigates alerts, and hunts for threats that slip past automated tools. Analysts triage the noise so you only hear about the things that are actually real, and they act to contain an attack before it spreads.

Full incident response

Complete includes end to end incident response at no extra cost, with no hourly caps, a dedicated response lead on confirmed incidents, and root cause analysis so the same attack does not come back. High severity cases carry a 60 minute response SLA on 90% of cases.

Works with what you run

More than 350 integrations across endpoint, network, cloud, identity, and email. It runs on Sophos endpoint protection or the lightweight XDR Sensor and ingests signal from third party tools like Microsoft 365, so there is no need to rip and replace what you already own.

You stay in control

Pick how much the team does for you with three threat response modes: Notify, Collaborate, or Authorize. Set it once in Sophos Central and change it whenever your needs change. Every tier also carries a breach protection warranty of up to $1 million.

MDR Essentials and MDR Complete share the same 24/7 monitoring and threat hunting. The difference is who finishes the job when a real attack lands. See how the two tiers stack up and pick the one that fits your team.
