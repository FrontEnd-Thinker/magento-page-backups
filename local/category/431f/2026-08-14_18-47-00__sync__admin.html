Fortinet 431F. Indoor Tri-Radio Wi-Fi 6 Access Point

The FortiAP 431F is an enterprise-class indoor Wi-Fi 6 (802.11ax) access point with a tri-radio, 4x4:4 MU-MIMO design that includes a dedicated scanning radio for security and RF monitoring. With OFDMA, internal antennas, and a 2.5GbE uplink port, it's built for high-density indoor spaces like large offices, classrooms, and conference centers that need next-generation Wi-Fi capacity. Note: the FortiAP 431F is an indoor-rated access point, not an outdoor or weatherproof unit — Fortinet's outdoor Wi-Fi 6 options live in the separate FortiAP-U series.

431F FAQ

The FortiAP 431F is an indoor access point with internal antennas and T-rail ceiling mounting. It is not rated for outdoor installation; Fortinet's outdoor Wi-Fi 6 access points are part of the separate FortiAP-U series.

The 431F supports 802.11ax, also known as Wi-Fi 6, with a tri-radio 4x4:4 MU-MIMO design including OFDMA and a dedicated scanning radio, while remaining backward compatible with 802.11ac and older clients.

The 431F can be powered by 802.3af or 802.3at PoE over its Ethernet ports, or by an optional 12V/2.5A power adapter if PoE isn't available. Its 2.5GbE port supports full throughput while the second GbE port adds PoE source diversity.

Yes. The 431F is managed centrally by the integrated WLAN controller on any FortiGate firewall, or through Fortinet's FortiLAN Cloud provisioning and management portal.

In addition to its 2.4GHz and 5GHz client radios, the 431F includes a dedicated dual-band scanning radio for continuous RF spectrum analysis and wireless intrusion detection, without interrupting client traffic.
