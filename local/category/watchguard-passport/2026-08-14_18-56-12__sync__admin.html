Passport at a Glance

DNS Protection

DNSWatchGO blocks malicious domains, phishing sites, and inappropriate content at the DNS layer, before connections reach the endpoint. Coverage follows the user, not the network, so it works whether they are in the office, at home, or on public Wi-Fi.

Multi-Factor Authentication

AuthPoint MFA prevents stolen credentials from being used to access VPNs, cloud apps, and Windows or Mac logins. Push-based approval through the AuthPoint mobile app keeps the experience simple for end users while closing a common attack path.

Threat Detection and Response

WatchGuard TDR correlates threat indicators from the endpoint agent, the firewall, and DNS to automate response and stop active attacks. Automated remediation actions reduce dwell time without requiring manual analyst intervention for every alert.

Remote Worker Coverage

All three services in Passport operate off-network. Users working from home, hotels, or coffee shops get the same DNS filtering, MFA enforcement, and threat response as they would inside the corporate perimeter, with no VPN split-tunnel workarounds required.

Passport FAQ

WatchGuard Passport is a per-user security bundle that combines three WatchGuard services into a single subscription: DNSWatchGO for DNS-layer filtering and content control, AuthPoint for multi-factor authentication, and Threat Detection and Response (TDR) for endpoint threat correlation and automated response. It is designed to protect users wherever they work, on or off the corporate network, all managed from WatchGuard Cloud.

Yes. All three services in Passport operate independently of the corporate network. DNSWatchGO routes DNS queries through WatchGuard's cloud filtering infrastructure, AuthPoint validates logins through a cloud-based MFA flow, and TDR monitors the endpoint agent directly. Remote and mobile workers receive the same protection as in-office users, with no requirement to be connected to a VPN or corporate gateway.

Passport includes three services. DNSWatchGO provides cloud DNS filtering and content control that blocks malicious domains and phishing sites before they reach the endpoint. AuthPoint provides multi-factor authentication for VPNs, cloud applications, and Windows and Mac logins. Threat Detection and Response (TDR) correlates threat data from the endpoint agent and firewall to automate detection and response actions.

WatchGuard Passport is managed from WatchGuard Cloud, the same unified console used for WatchGuard firewalls and other security services. Administrators configure policies, monitor activity, and review alerts for all three Passport services from a single interface. No on-premises management server or dedicated appliance is required.

Passport is licensed on a per-user, annual subscription basis. Each license covers one user and includes all three bundled services (DNSWatchGO, AuthPoint, and TDR) for the full subscription term. Multi-year terms are available and can reduce the per-user cost. Contact Firewalls.com for volume pricing on larger deployments.
