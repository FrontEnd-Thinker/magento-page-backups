FortiWiFi 70G / 71G FAQ

The hardware platform is identical. Both run Fortinet's SP5 SD-WAN ASIC and deliver the same throughput: 10 Gbps firewall, 2.5 Gbps IPS, 1.5 Gbps NGFW, and 1.3 Gbps threat protection. The FortiWiFi 71G adds a 64 GB onboard SSD for local log storage and reporting. If your branch needs on-box traffic history and compliance records without a separate FortiAnalyzer, the 71G is the right pick. Both models include built-in dual-band Wi-Fi 6.

The FortiWiFi 70G and 71G include a dual-band, dual-stream 802.11ax (Wi-Fi 6) access point built directly into the chassis, covering both 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz bands. For a small branch this often eliminates the need for a separate access point. For larger spaces or higher client density, the integrated wireless controller manages up to 32 external FortiAP units alongside the built-in radio, all from the same FortiOS interface.

Most branch deployments choose UTP (Unified Threat Protection), which bundles IPS, anti-malware, web filtering, DNS filtering, and antispam with FortiCare Premium support. Sites with stricter compliance requirements often step up to Enterprise Protection, which adds DLP and AI inline malware prevention. ATP covers the core threat prevention stack without antispam and FortiCare Premium. Our engineers can recommend the right bundle once we know your user count, traffic mix, and compliance obligations.
