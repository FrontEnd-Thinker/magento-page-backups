Adaptive Endpoint Protection Plus at a Glance

NGAV Core Protection

Inherits the full Adaptive Endpoint Protection antivirus engine, including Collective Intelligence cloud lookups, behavioral heuristics, exploit prevention, and ransomware detection. Everything in AEP is included in AEP Plus.

URL Filtering

Block or monitor web access by category across more than 60 URL classifications, including adult content, gambling, social media, malware distribution sites, and phishing pages. Policies can be set per user group and enforced on or off the corporate network.

Device Control

Restrict which types of removable media and peripheral devices can interact with protected endpoints. Block unauthorized USB drives, optical media, and Bluetooth connections by policy, preventing both malware introduction and unauthorized data removal.

Unified Aether Management

Web filtering rules and device access policies are configured and enforced from the same Aether console that manages antivirus settings. Administrators maintain a single security policy view across all features, all platforms, and all device locations.

Adaptive Endpoint Protection Plus FAQ

Adaptive Endpoint Protection Plus adds two key capabilities on top of the base AEP antivirus engine: URL filtering and device control. URL filtering lets you block or restrict web access by content category, protecting users from phishing sites, malware distribution URLs, and non-work-related content. Device control lets you define which removable storage devices and peripherals are allowed to interact with your endpoints, reducing the risk of data theft or malware introduction via USB and similar media.

Yes. Because AEP Plus is cloud-managed, URL filtering policies apply to devices wherever they connect to the internet. Remote employees, home office users, and traveling staff are covered by the same web filtering rules as in-office machines, without needing to route their traffic through a corporate VPN or proxy server. Policy enforcement is handled by the local agent using cloud-based category lookups.

Yes. AEP Plus device control supports granular exception rules. You can whitelist specific device types, manufacturers, or individual device IDs while blocking all others. For example, you can allow a specific model of encrypted USB drive issued by your organization while blocking all other removable media. These exceptions are configured per policy and apply to the user groups assigned to that policy.

AEP Plus is well-suited for education environments where web content filtering is a compliance requirement (CIPA in the US, for example) and device control helps prevent students from copying data to unauthorized USB drives. The cloud-managed Aether console allows IT administrators to manage protection policies for student and staff devices from a single interface, regardless of whether devices are on campus or at home.
