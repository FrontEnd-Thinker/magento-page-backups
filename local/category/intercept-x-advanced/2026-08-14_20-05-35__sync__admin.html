Intercept X Advanced at a Glance

What's Included

Deep learning malware detection, exploit prevention, and CryptoGuard anti-ransomware, plus foundational controls like application control, web filtering, and device control. Advanced covers prevention. Advanced with XDR adds endpoint detection and response and cross-product threat hunting.

How It's Licensed

Licensed per user, not per device. One user license covers the reasonable number of computers that person signs into. Pricing is tiered by seat band, so buying into a higher band lowers your cost per seat. Education, health, and government buyers may qualify for per-device licensing.

Management

Managed entirely from Sophos Central, the cloud console. There are no servers to stand up. Default policies apply protection from day one, and the same console manages your Sophos firewalls, servers, and email if you run them.

Platforms and Servers

Protects Windows and macOS desktops and laptops. Servers use a separate Intercept X Advanced for Server subscription, licensed per machine. Mobile devices are covered by Sophos Intercept X for Mobile.

Advanced or Advanced with XDR?

Advanced stops attacks. Advanced with XDR adds detection, investigation, and response across your endpoints, servers, firewall, and email. See the full Intercept X lineup and pick the tier that fits your team.
