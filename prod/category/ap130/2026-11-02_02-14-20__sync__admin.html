The WatchGuard AP130 is a Wifi 6, 4 internal antenna, 1 GbE port, and 802.3ax PoE+ power indoor access point. Powered by a cloud based management systems, the WatchGuard AP130 delivers 22 dBm and a 3dBi antenna gain with only one ethernet port. This wifi 6 indoor access point, AP130, is best for low-density indoor environments that include smaller offices or remote workers.
