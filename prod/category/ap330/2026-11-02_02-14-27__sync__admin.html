The WatchGuard AP330 is a Wifi 6, 6 internal antenna, 2.5 GbE port, and a PoE+ power indoor access point. Powered by a cloud based management systems, the WatchGuard AP330 delivers 22 dBm and a 2dBi antenna gain with only one ethernet port. This wifi 6 indoor access point, AP330, is best for mid-density indoor environments of all shapes and sizes.
