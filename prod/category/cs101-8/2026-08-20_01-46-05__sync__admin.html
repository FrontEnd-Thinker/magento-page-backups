<p>Sophos Switch CS101-8FP is a cloud-managed network access layer switch designed for small to medium sized businesses (SMBs), remote offices, and retail environments. This model features 8 Gigabit Ethernet (10/100/1000Base-T) ports with full Power over Ethernet (PoE) capabilities, supporting both 802.3af and 802.3at standards, and offers a total PoE budget of 110W. It also includes 2 SFP 1G uplink ports for fiber connectivity.</p>
