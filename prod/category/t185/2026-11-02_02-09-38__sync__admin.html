T185 at a Glance

Performance

Firewall throughput (IMIX) up to 5.70 Gbps and (UDP 1518) up to 7.90 Gbps. Supports 3,850,000 concurrent connections. The T185 is the highest-capacity model in the Firebox T Series tabletop lineup, built for branch offices and retail environments with heavier-than-average traffic demands.

Connectivity

Ships with 4 x 1 GbE and 4 x 2.5 GbE ports, including two PoE+ PSE ports (802.3at), plus 1 x SFP/SFP+ for fiber uplinks. Optional port expansion modules add integrated fiber connectivity as your network requirements grow, without replacing the appliance.

Security Services

UTM throughput (full scan) up to 1.83 Gbps. Antivirus throughput 2.80 Gbps. IPS (full scan) 2.40 Gbps. HTTPS inspection with IPS enabled at 1.00 Gbps. Full security stack includes AI-powered anti-malware, DNS filtering, and threat correlation through WatchGuard's ThreatSync platform.

Management

Managed locally via Fireware Web UI or WatchGuard System Manager, or centrally through WatchGuard Cloud. Includes SD-WAN with dynamic path selection, High Availability (active/passive, active/active), and over 100 pre-built dashboards and compliance reports at no additional cost.
