Why Sophos

What Sets Sophos Apart

One console for everything

Sophos Central manages endpoint, server, email, mobile, encryption, and firewall from a single web console. You get one login and one place to set policy instead of stitching together separate tools from different vendors.

AI that blocks what signatures miss

Deep learning inspects file attributes from hundreds of millions of samples to catch malware it has never seen before. CryptoGuard anti-ransomware detects malicious encryption and rolls affected files back to a safe state.

Synchronized Security

Sophos products share threat intelligence and act as one system. A detection on a mobile device or endpoint can automatically restrict its access to the network until the threat is cleared, without an admin lifting a finger.

Expert help on your terms

Keep threat hunting in-house with XDR, or hand it off entirely with Sophos MDR, where a team of analysts monitors and responds around the clock. You choose how much you manage and how much Sophos does for you.
