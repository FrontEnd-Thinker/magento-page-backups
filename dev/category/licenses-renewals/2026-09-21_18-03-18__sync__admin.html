Advanced Gateway Security Suite (AGSS) is SonicWall's comprehensive security subscription bundle for firewall appliances. It includes Gateway Anti-Virus, Intrusion Prevention, Application Control, Content Filtering Service, Capture ATP sandboxing, and 24x7 support in a single subscription. AGSS is the most popular license bundle for organizations that want complete protection managed through one renewal.

When a SonicWall security subscription expires, the firewall hardware continues to function but the associated security services stop updating. Gateway Anti-Virus signatures, Capture ATP submissions, Content Filtering category lookups, and IPS updates all depend on an active subscription. Letting a license lapse leaves your network exposed to new threats. Firewalls.com recommends renewing before expiration to avoid any gap in protection.

Yes. SonicWall allows customers to renew lapsed subscriptions, though SonicWall may require co-term adjustments depending on how long the license has been expired. Our licensing team at Firewalls.com handles lapsed renewal orders regularly and can walk you through the process to get your subscriptions reinstated quickly.

SSL VPN licenses enable browser-based remote access using SonicWall's NetExtender or web-based portal, which works without a pre-installed client on the remote device. Global VPN Client (GVC) licenses use IPsec and require the SonicWall GVC client application installed on the user's machine. SSL VPN is easier to deploy for casual remote users, while GVC is preferred for permanent remote workers who need a consistent, full-tunnel VPN connection.
