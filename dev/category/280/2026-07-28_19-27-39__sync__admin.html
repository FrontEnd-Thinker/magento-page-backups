TZ280 at a Glance

The TZ280 is the Gen 8 successor to the TZ270 (Gen 7), running SonicOS 8 instead of SonicOS 7. It ships with TLS 1.3 inspection, the SonicOS 8 interface, NSM 3.0 management, Cloud Secure Edge connector support, and an embedded Cysurance cyber warranty (up to $200K) on any unit registered with an active security suite. The TZ280 keeps the same 8x 1GbE + 2x 1G SFP layout as the TZ270 but adds higher max connections (1M SPI / 200K DPI) and 1 Gbps threat prevention throughput.

Essential Protection includes Capture ATP (RTDMI sandbox), Gateway Anti-Virus, Anti-Spyware, IPS, Application Control, Content Filtering, Anti-Spam, and 24x7 support. Advanced Protection adds DNS Filtering, Cloud App Security, and NSM Essential cloud management. The Advanced suite also activates the embedded Cysurance warranty. TotalSecure and Secure Upgrade Plus are bundle SKUs that package the firewall hardware with either suite for a fixed 1, 2, 3, or 5 year term.

Appliance Only is a single TZ280 with no security services attached. You bring your own license or buy one separately. The High Availability SKU is a secondary TZ280 sold at a discount, designed to pair with a primary TZ280 in Active/Standby mode with stateful failover. The HA unit cannot run as a standalone firewall. It only operates as a backup to a registered primary. You also need matching security licenses on both units for full failover coverage.

One sizing note: if you are replacing a TZ370 because you outgrew it, step up to a TZ380 to keep the same performance class. Step down to a TZ280 only if you have measured your traffic and it sits well inside its envelope. Our team will quote both options if you are not sure.

The TZ280 launched as part of SonicWall's Gen 8 TZ refresh in 2025, alongside the TZ80, TZ380, TZ480, TZ580, and TZ680. All TZ280 variants are shipping and stocked for same day delivery on most configurations when ordered by 3pm EST.

The TZ280 and TZ380 share the same desktop chassis and 8x 1GbE port count, but the TZ380 jumps from 1 Gbps to 1.5 Gbps threat prevention throughput, swaps the 1G SFP ports for 5/2.5/1G SFP+, and adds optional redundant power and expandable storage up to 512 GB. Step up to the TZ380 if you need more than 1 Gbps of inspected throughput, redundant PSU for uptime requirements, onboard log storage for compliance, or ISP service faster than 1 Gbps that should land on multigig SFP+ uplinks.
