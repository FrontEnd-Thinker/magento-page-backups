Why the TZ Series

What Sets the TZ Apart

Deep Packet Inspection

Every TZ inspects every byte of every packet without proxying or reassembly. Gen 8 models inspect encrypted TLS 1.3 traffic at wire speed. No other vendor at this price point delivers the same inspection depth across the entire product line.

SD-WAN Built In

SD-WAN comes standard on every TZ at no additional license cost. Route traffic across multiple ISP connections using application aware and performance based policies without a separate appliance.

Zero Touch Deployment

Ship a TZ to any remote site and have it pull its configuration from the cloud automatically. No local IT staff required. Network Security Manager lets you deploy, monitor, and update every TZ from one dashboard.

Desktop Form Factor

Every TZ model fits on a desk, shelf, or standard rack with a 1U mount kit. The smaller models operate without fans, keeping noise at zero for open office environments where server room noise is not an option.

The Advanced Protection Service Suite (APSS) adds DNS Filtering, Cloud App Security, and NSM Essential cloud management on top of everything in EPSS. Both are available in 1, 2, 3, and 5 year terms. You can purchase them bundled with hardware as TotalSecure SKUs or as standalone renewals. Contact us for a side by side quote comparing both tiers on any TZ model.

Every TZ supports site to site VPN, IPSec remote access VPN, and SSL VPN out of the box. You can add user packs in various increments if you need more sessions than the base license provides. SSL VPN licenses are perpetual and do not expire with your security services subscription.
