Capture Client Advanced at a Glance

NGAV and Threat Prevention

SentinelOne's AI engine stops malware, ransomware, and exploits before they run. Signature-free detection means protection stays current without waiting for daily definition updates or a scheduled scan window to complete.

Web Content Filtering

Enforce internet access policy on every managed endpoint, whether the device is on the office network, at home, or on public wi-fi. Block by category, maintain a custom URL list, or restrict access by time of day. Policy is pushed from the cloud console, no VPN tunnel required.

DPI-SSL Certificate Management

Automatically deploys and manages the root certificates your SonicWall firewall needs to inspect encrypted HTTPS traffic. Eliminates the manual IT work of pushing certificates to each machine before enabling DPI-SSL on the firewall.

Centralized Cloud Management

Manage every protected endpoint from a single SonicWall cloud console. Set policy, view endpoint status, review alerts, and generate reports from any browser. No on-premises management server to maintain or patch.

Capture Client Advanced is SonicWall's mid-tier endpoint protection product. It provides SentinelOne-powered next-generation antivirus, web content filtering for endpoints on or off the corporate network, and DPI-SSL certificate management to simplify encrypted traffic inspection on SonicWall firewalls. Everything is managed from the SonicWall cloud console.
