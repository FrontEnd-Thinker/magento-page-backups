TZ280W at a Glance

Performance

2.5 Gbps firewall inspection, 1 Gbps threat prevention, 1.5 Gbps IPS throughput, 430 Mbps DPI-SSL. Supports 12,000 new connections per second and up to 1,000,000 concurrent SPI connections.

Wi-Fi 6 Wireless

Built-in dual-band 802.11ax (Wi-Fi 6) radio with 2x2 MIMO, supporting 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz bands. WPA3 encryption, virtual access point segmentation, WIDS/WIPS, and wireless guest services with captive portal.

Wired Interfaces

Eight 1GbE copper ports, two 1G SFP uplink slots, USB Type-A for cellular failover, and USB Type-C console port. Expandable storage up to 512 GB via optional module.

Security Services

Gateway Anti-Virus, Anti-Spyware, IPS, Application Control, Content Filtering Service 5.0, Capture ATP with RTDMI, DNS Security, Advanced DNS Filtering, and Comprehensive Anti-Spam. Active subscription required.

TZ280W FAQ

The TZ280W includes a built-in Wi-Fi 6 (802.11ax) radio with dual-band support for 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz. It supports WPA3 encryption, 2x2 MIMO, and can create multiple virtual access points (VAPs) for network segmentation.

The TZ380W offers higher throughput at 3.5 Gbps firewall inspection versus 2.5 Gbps for the TZ280W, and includes two multi-gig SFP+ ports supporting 5G/2.5G/1G versus the TZ280W two 1G SFP ports. The TZ380W is suited for larger small offices with faster uplink requirements.
